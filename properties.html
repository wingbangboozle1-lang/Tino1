How to use this page

  1. Use Property A's rounded values only to compare live, rent, and sell choices; the projections are not an appraisal or tax bill.
  2. Keep Property B in the model as an already-owned asset. Its rent, utilities, insurance, and tax history are not inheritance-tax inputs for Property A.
  3. Before the transfer, have the attorney and CPA verify the trust, gross-estate inclusion, title, basis, tax-rate areas, insurance classification, and all operating bills offline. See Flagged for Review.

Official sources below explain the legal treatment and agency methods; they do not verify the private parcel figures. The page follows the $10 publication rule while preserving uncertainty instead of inventing a value.
