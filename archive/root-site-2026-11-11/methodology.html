Methodology

Exactly how every number on this site was produced — so you can audit it, reproduce it, or replace its inputs with better ones.

1 · Ground rules (non-negotiable, inherited from the project brief)

  1. Privacy first. No names, addresses, APNs, trust names, financial institution names or parcel identifiers anywhere. Placeholders: [Property A], [Property B], [Transferor], [Heir], [Family] Trust. User-supplied dollar figures are rounded to the nearest $10 before display — including the historical assessment series (this is why the owner-supplied anchors render $10-rounded throughout.). Statutory constants set by law stay exact ($15,000,000; $19,000; $1,044,586) because rounding law is not privacy protection, it is falsification.
  2. Precision by rounding. All reported dollars, taxes, values and projections are multiples of $10 (values computed unrounded, displayed rounded). Rates and percentages shown to natural precision. Intermediate CSV stores full precision; display pages show $10.
  3. Source or silence. Every factual claim links an official source (federal statute, CA statute, agency PDF/page, official statistics). What could not be confirmed is left blank or labelled and sent to Flagged for Review — never filled in by inference.
  4. Assumptions are opinions with labels. Appreciation, inflation, returns, growth caps and holding costs are the analyst’s chosen inputs, always tagged assumption with their anchor named (FHFA, BLS, FOMC, Treasury, BOE).
  5. Nominal, deterministic, pre-tax-ish. Projections are deterministic compounding — no Monte Carlo, no fake confidence intervals; distributions live in the regime grid (Black swans) instead. Nominal dollars throughout; ordinary income tax on rents excluded (applies equally to rent scenarios); capital-gains tax included at ≈ 37.1% combined.

3 · The formulas (all public-record arithmetic)

  • Prop 13 assessed path (pre-death): assessed(t) = assessed(t−1) × (1 + r), r = min(2%, CA CPI change) as supplied by the county — modelled 2% flat.
  • Prop 19 excluded amount: excluded = FBYV + cap(t). New taxable value: taxable = max(FBYV, FMV(t) − cap(t)). FBYV(2045) = 550,540 × 1.02¹⁹ ≈ $802,030; cap(2045) = 1,044,590 × 1.02¹⁹ ≈ $1,521,770; taxable = max(802,030, 5,959,750 − 1,521,770) = $4,437,980; tax = 1.1636% × 4,437,980 + direct ≈ $53,320. If rented/no live-in: taxable = FMV = $5,959,750; tax ≈ $71,030.
  • B reassessment (no exclusion possible for rentals): taxable = FMV(2045) = $3,156,310; tax = 1.1816% × 3,156,310 + fixed ≈ $39,050.
  • Estate exclusion path: excl(t) = 15,000,000 × 1.025^(t−2026), t ≥ 2026.
  • Terminal wealth (2060, nominal): W(S) = Σ invested flows × (1+r_port) compounded + Σ (liquidation net of selling costs) − gains tax on (liquidation value − date-of-death basis). Step-up basis at 2045 = FMV(2045). Gains tax = 0.371 × max(0, V₂₀₆₀ − basis). S1 seeds the account with A’s net sale proceeds at 2045.
  • GFC shock path: appreciation shocked −7.5% (A) / −7.0% (B) for 2045–49, then +4.5% both; portfolio −10% (2046–49) then +9%; rent +0.5%.
  • Black-swan parameters: see Black swans table — all are deliberately explicit, none are “90% confidence”.

4 · Privacy audit (this site and the whole repository)

  • Zero names/addresses/APNs/trust identifiers anywhere in the canonical pages (grep-verified). Legacy pages scrubbed 2026-11-11: every user-supplied figure rounded to $10 in docs/…, site/…, properties.html, dashboard/ (already withheld), model/, research/, data/, analysis/data/ — automated with integrated/scrub.py (exact literal map, ROUND_HALF_UP, no regex guessing).
  • Year-by-year assessment history shown only in $10-rounded form on Properties; exact values no longer appear anywhere in the repo.
  • Statutory constants kept exact by design (P15) — they identify nothing about the owner.
  • Git history still contains earlier commits with exact figures (e.g., merge 2a16da8 and before). True erasure would require history rewriting — flagged for the owner to decide: git filter-repo is destructive and force-pushes; not performed unilaterally. (See REPO-REVIEW.md §privacy.)

6 · What this analysis is not

Not legal advice (attorney required for the deeds/trust administration), not tax advice (CPA required for the 706/709/540 filings), not investment advice (fiduciary required for reinvestment), not an appraisal (certified appraiser required for basis), not insurance advice (broker required for the FAIR Plan decision), and not an estate plan. It is an organized evidence pack that makes all five cheaper.

7 · Known limitations

  • Single-path deterministic compounding; regime grid is the honesty valve (Black swans).
  • Ordinary-income tax on rents omitted; would widen S1’s advantage.
  • Insurance premiums are placeholders pending quotes.
  • Inheritance year = 2045; every figure scales mildly with earlier/later death (direction unchanged).
  • Exchange rates, leverage, 1031 (not applicable at death), GRATs — out of scope; see Estate tax §5.
