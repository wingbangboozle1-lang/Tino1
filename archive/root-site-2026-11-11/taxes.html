4 · Local & misc.

  • No city inheritance/estate/gift tax exists in Santa Clara, Cupertino, SF or any CA jurisdiction (R&TC §13301; Cal. Const.). “Property tax reassessment” is the only death-triggered local tax.
  • Direct charges / parcel taxes (A: school/college/library ≈ $930/yr 2025; B: fixed parcel taxes ≈ $1,000/yr) are not capped by Prop 13’s 2% and grow independently — they are already baked into the projections.
  • Mello-Roos/CFD: none identified for either property (would appear itemised on the bill; verify when it arrives post-death).
  • Supplemental bills: after reassessment, SF and SCC both issue supplemental tax bills for the gap period — budget one extra ≈ 0.5-year bill in year 1 (≈ $19,500 / $26,000+; often overlooked).
  • Form 4868/4768 analogs: none at death; only 706 timelines matter (9 months; 4768 extension 6 months).
