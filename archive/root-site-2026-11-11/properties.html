What each property does for the plan

  • [Property A] (inherits via trust): a $3.1M→$5.96M (2045) asset with a $550,540 Prop 13 base. Inheriting it properly = the entire tax game. See Inheritance steps, Live / Rent / Sell.
  • [Property B] (already owned by the heir): provides (i) rental income covering both properties’ carry, (ii) a zero-rent primary residence (third unit) as part of any plan, (iii) a Prop 13 base absurdly below market — which is why selling B is almost never optimal. Note B itself is not part of the inheritance; it is the control property the analysis must keep in view.
San Francisco rental law context (verified): if B was built before 14 Jun 1979, the two occupied units are under rent control (1.6% max increase 2026–27, just-cause eviction); voluntary vacancy resets to market. Empty Homes Tax was struck down by the Court of Appeal (2026-09-11) and is not collected — could be revived on review; keep units occupied. SF rental laws · 2026–27 increase · SF EHT status
