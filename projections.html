Assessed value & Prop 19 cap mechanics (the two clocks everyone misses)

  1. Prop 13 clock (pre-death): A and B assessed bases grow ≤ the California CPI change, max 2%/yr (Cal. Const. XIII A §2(b)); drops allowed (Prop 8) in declines. The bases shown for 2014–2026 grew ≈ 2%/yr every year except 2020 (+1%) exactly as the constitution permits.
  2. Prop 19 clock (post-death): for the transferor’s principal residence transferred to a child, the excluded amount = FBYV + cap; the cap was $1,000,000 (2021) → adjusted every 2 years by FHFA CA HPI → $1,044,590 for transfers 2/16/2025–2/15/2027 (BOE LTA 2025/009). Model: +2%/yr thereafter. If full cash value ≤ FBYV + cap, no reassessed excess; otherwise the excess is added to the FBYV. File BOE-19-P within 3 years; claim Homeowners’ Exemption (BOE-266) within 1 year or the exclusion is lost — no exceptions (LTA 2026/026 Q7).
  3. Multi-unit rule: only the unit the Transferor occupied qualifies; B’s other units are reassessed to market regardless. If B were the principal residence, its third unit (or a unit) could be the exclusion property instead — confirm where the Homeowners’ Exemption is claimed now.
  4. Prop 8 decline-in-value: if market falls below factored base, assessed value drops to market (both properties currently trade far above their bases — no relief available today).
