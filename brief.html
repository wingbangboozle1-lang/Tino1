Questions for the estate attorney

  1. Will the trust-held Property A be in the Transferor's gross estate, so that the basis resets at death? flag Q01 Rev. Rul. 2023-2 · IRS inherited-property FAQ
  2. Was a Form 706 filed for a predeceased spouse, and can a portability election still be made? flag Q02 IRS estate FAQ
  3. Does the trust transfer meet the Proposition 19 parent-child definitions, and who files BOE-19-P, and when? flag Q06 BOE LTA 2026/026 · BOE Pub. 800-1
  4. Is Property A the Transferor's principal residence, with the homeowners' exemption on file? flag Q15 BOE homeowners' exemption · R&TC 63.2

Questions for the CPA

  1. Basis: is the date-of-death appraisal the basis, and is Form 8971 needed? Property B has no reset. flag Q03 IRS inherited-property FAQ
  2. Rental years: depreciation over 27.5 years, and recapture on sale at up to 25% federal. IRS Pub. 527 · IRS Topic 409
  3. Lived-in path: the Section 121 two-of-five-years test before any sale. IRS Topic 701
  4. Net investment income tax on rent and gains above the thresholds. IRS Topic 559
  5. Parked proceeds: interest is taxed each year at ordinary rates; stock gains at sale. IRS Topic 409
  6. California: no estate or inheritance tax return for deaths since 2005; confirm the trust's state income reporting. CA Controller
