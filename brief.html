4. Questions for the estate attorney

  1. Trust type and whether Property A is inside the gross estate, for the basis reset (Q01). Rev. Rul. 2023-2
  2. Total estate and any ported spousal exclusion (Q02); whether a Form 706 is needed. IRS estate FAQ
  3. Relationship to the Inheritor under the Prop. 19 definitions (Q06). BOE Pub. 800-1
  4. When title actually passes and whether a marital or community-property rule applies (Q16). BOE Prop 19
  5. Who files BOE-266 and BOE-19-P, and in what order, within one year and three years (S03). BOE Pub. 800-1
  6. Timing of trustee notice (60 days) and the change-in-ownership statement (150 days). Prob. Code 16061.7 · R&TC 480

5. Questions for the tax adviser

  1. Filing status and bracket for the Inheritor (Q05); the investment-income tax threshold. IRS Topic 559
  2. Property B: purchase basis, depreciation taken and the 25% recapture rate (Q03). IRS Pub. 527 · IRS Topic 409
  3. Which home has the homeowners' exemption now (Q18); $7,000 of taxable value. BOE homeowners' exemption
  4. Deductibility of selling costs on a prompt sale of Property A (Q17). IRS Topic 409
  5. Consistent basis: Form 706 value and the Form 8971 report (inherited basis). IRS inherited-property FAQ
  6. Home-sale exclusion for Property B if it is ever sold ($250,000 single; 2 of 5 years). IRS Topic 701

6. Deadlines (full timeline: Deadline timeline)

Within 60 days after a revocable trust becomes irrevocable at death: trustee notice. Within 150 days of death: change-in-ownership statement. Within 9 months: Form 706 if required (Form 4768 adds six months). Within 1 year: move in and file BOE-266 if living there. Within 3 years: BOE-19-P. Sale year: Form 8949 and Schedule D. Each year: rent reporting and the rent cap notice.
