Mixed paths - not only the three pure choices

A real plan can mix the choices: live in Property A for a while, then sell; or rent it for a while, then sell. Because the basis reset applies to a prompt sale, waiting longer exposes any further gain to tax - so mixed paths sit between the pure outcomes.

Reinvestment risk beside the headline. The headline plan - sell Property A and invest the proceeds in stocks - assumes the money earns a stock-like return. If the proceeds are parked in cash or Treasury bills instead (about 3.5% a year), renting comes out ahead ($5,582,450 vs $3,554,580 in 2026 dollars by 2076). That is why the plan says do not park the money. The phased-investing path on the Black swan page tests the middle ground: 24-36 months in 3M-2Y Treasuries (~4.4%), then phased into stocks, which softens 1929/2008 crash entry risk but gives up some upside if stocks keep rising.
