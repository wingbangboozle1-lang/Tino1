Step-by-step process for the inherited home

What has to be done, by whom and by when, from today through the first tax return after the inheritance.

Steps and deadlines for Property A
StepWhenWhat to doWhy it matters hereOfficial basis
1Now, while the Transferor is aliveHave an estate attorney confirm what kind of trust holds Property A and whether the home will be included in the Transferor's gross estate.The basis reset, and with it the whole case for selling, depends on it. Assets of an irrevocable grantor trust outside the estate get no reset. flag Q01Rev. Rul. 2023-2 · IRS inherited-property FAQ
2NowKeep Property A as the Transferor's principal residence with the homeowners' exemption on file.The parent-child exclusion applies only to a home that was the Transferor's principal residence.BOE Prop 19 · BOE homeowners' exemption
3NowTotal the expected estate: Property A, cash and everything else the Transferor owns. Property B is not part of it.Shows how much room is left under the federal exclusion; see Cash and estate tax. flag Q02IRS estate tax
4Date of deathRecord the date. Order a written appraisal of Property A as of that date.For a revocable trust this is the change-in-ownership date, and the appraisal sets the new basis.BOE Rule 462.260 · IRS inherited-property FAQ
5Within 60 daysThe successor trustee serves the notification to every beneficiary and heir.Required when a revocable trust becomes irrevocable by death; starts the 120-day contest period.Prob. Code 16061.7
6Soon after deathGet an employer identification number for the trust or estate; file Form 1041 if it has more than $600 of gross income in a year.Rent, interest or dividends received before distribution are taxed to the trust or passed through.IRS estate income tax
7Within 150 days of deathFile the change-in-ownership statement for Property A with the Santa Clara County Assessor.Statutory deadline when ownership changes by death; a penalty applies after a written request is ignored.R&TC 480
8Within 9 months of deathFile Form 706 if the gross estate plus adjusted taxable gifts is above the filing threshold, or to elect portability for a surviving spouse. Form 4768 gives six more months.Above the threshold the tax is 40% of the excess. Portability is lost without a timely return.IRS estate FAQ · Form 706 instructions
9Decide early: live, rent or sellUse the Decision page. If selling, list promptly and keep the home insured while empty.A sale near the date-of-death value has almost no taxable gain. Vacant homes can lose cover after 60 days.IRS inherited-property FAQ · CDI residential guide
10Within 1 year, only if moving inMake Property A the Inheritor's principal residence and file for the homeowners' exemption there.Without both, the Proposition 19 exclusion is not available and the home is reassessed at full market value.R&TC 63.2 · BOE LTA 2026/026
11Within 3 years, only if moving inFile the parent-child exclusion claim (form BOE-19-P) with the Assessor, before any transfer to a third party.Later claims give relief only going forward.BOE Prop 19 · Santa Clara Assessor
12After the Assessor actsExpect a supplemental tax bill for Property A covering the period from the date of death.Budget for it in every choice. The San Francisco page describes the same California process. flag U06SF Treasurer
13Tax year of the sale, if soldReport the sale on Form 8949 and Schedule D using the date-of-death value as basis; match any Schedule A to Form 8971 received.A basis above the value finally determined for estate tax can draw an accuracy penalty.IRS inherited-property FAQ · IRS Topic 409
14Every year, if rentedReport rent and expenses; depreciate the building part of the new basis over 27.5 years. Follow the state rent cap and its notice rules.Depreciation shelters part of the rent but is taxed back at up to 25% federal on a later sale.IRS Pub. 527 · IRS Topic 409 · CA Attorney General
15When the cash arrivesNothing to report as income for the inheritance itself. Income earned on it afterwards is taxable. Inherited retirement accounts follow different rules.The cash is taxed, if at all, to the estate before it is paid out, not to the Inheritor.IRS Pub. 559
16ThroughoutLeave Property B exactly as it is: same owner, same exemption or rental status.Any transfer of Property B could trigger its own reassessment; nothing about the inheritance requires one.BOE Prop 19 · SF Treasurer

Deadlines are quoted from the linked statutes and agency pages as read on 2026-11-11. Forms and county procedures change; confirm each one with the county office and the attorney handling the trust.
