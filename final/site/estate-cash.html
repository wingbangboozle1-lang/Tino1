What this means for the cash

  • There is no city, county or California tax on the inheritance. The only threshold that matters is the federal one, and it is measured on the Transferor's whole estate: Property A, the cash and everything else. Property B is not counted.
  • With inheritance in 2045 the baseline leaves about $17,612,280 of room for cash and other assets ($11,016,970 in 2026 dollars). Above that, each extra dollar is taxed at 40%.
  • If the Transferor is a surviving spouse and a timely Form 706 elected portability when the first spouse died, the room is roughly doubled. This needs to be checked now, because a missed election can sometimes still be made within five years of the first death.
  • Selling, renting or living in Property A does not change the estate tax. It is fixed at the date of death.
  • The Inheritor does not report the cash as income. Interest, dividends and gains earned on it afterwards are taxable, and inherited retirement accounts are taxed as they are withdrawn.
