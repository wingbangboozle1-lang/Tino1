Bottom line (2045 midpoint inheritance)

Pure financial return favors selling Cupertino at inheritance and investing ($5,110,000 net → ~$10,050,000 in 10 yrs @7% vs ~$7,310,000 if kept @3% home growth). Renting Cupertino is the weakest option (~$40,880/yr net on $5,440,000 equity ≈ 0.8% yield, plus full reassessment tax ~$72,580/yr). Living in Cupertino keeps Prop 19 partial protection (tax ~$55,690/yr vs ~$72,580/yr) but still faces a ~5× tax jump vs today — the single biggest cost driver. See Scenarios for the full math and caveats (imputed rent, lifestyle value, investment taxes).
  • California charges no inheritance tax — the tax risk is federal estate tax (only above $15M/person) and property-tax reassessment under Prop 19, not a death tax. Details →
  • Step-up basis (IRC §1014) resets your basis to market value at death — selling promptly after inheritance means near-zero capital gains. Waiting lets gains (up to ~37% combined fed+CA) accrue. Details →
  • SF property is the income engine: $85,800/yr rent (2026) vs $22,610/yr costs. Keep it in all scenarios; its rental status means no Prop 19 protection at transfer (full reassessment). Details →
  • Solar credit is fragile: the $10–$20/mo profit assumes grandfathered NEM 2.0. Under NEM 3.0 (post-Apr 2023) exports pay ~75% less — verify the interconnection date. Flagged →
