Before any decision can reasonably be made

The register holds 22 question and data flags, 47 repository review findings and the assumption flags A01-A10. These change the answer; the rest are refinements.

  • flag Q01 What kind of trust holds Property A, and will the home be included in the Transferor's gross estate? Without the basis reset, selling loses its advantage over renting.
  • flag Q02 How large is the estate, and was a deceased spouse's unused exclusion ported? This sets the room before federal estate tax applies.
  • flag U04 What will the Proposition 19 allowance be for a transfer after 2027-02-16? The official figure is not published yet.
  • flag A01 Growth, inflation and return assumptions: the ranking between renting and selling is sensitive to these over a 50-year horizon.
