## 4. San Francisco Rent Ordinance (`94122`) & Maintenance / Selling Costs

1. **San Francisco Rent Ordinance (`SF Admin. Code Ch. 37`) & Rent Board Fee:**
   - Annual allowable rent increase for covered multi-unit buildings (pre-June 1979, typical of Outer Sunset `94122` triplexes): **`1.4%`** (3/1/2025–2/28/2026) and **`1.6%`** (3/1/2026–2/28/2027), calculated at **60% of Bay Area CPI**! Only upon voluntary vacancy/turnover do rents reset to market (`Costa-Hawkins Rental Housing Act`, `Cal. Civ. Code § 1954.50`). Modeling blended SF rent growth (in-place 60%-of-CPI + periodic 5–7 yr unit turnover) yields **`~2.8%/yr`** long-run rent growth.
   - **SF Rent Board Fee (`$59/unit/yr`, 50% banking/recovery from tenants):** Billed **directly by the SF Rent Board starting in 2022** rather than on the secured property tax bill — **explaining the exact `-5.9%` (`-$131`) drop in `[Property B]`'s property tax bill in 2021–2022!** (`3 units × $59 = $180/yr` rounded to `$10`, or `$90/yr` net landlord share if 50% recouped on rented units).
   - **Official Sources:** [SF Rent Board Current Rates (2026–2027)](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) & [SF Rent Board Fee Portal](https://www.sf.gov/departments--rent-board).
2. **Maintenance & Capital Reserve Allowance (Structure Value Basis, Not Land Value):**
   - Because Bay Area land represents 65%–80% of total market value, applying a naive "1% of home price" rule (`$31,000/yr` on `$3.1M`) overstates maintenance, whereas ignoring roof/HVAC/solar/exterior capex understates it. Using structure replacement value (`~0.75%` of Cupertino structure / `~0.25%` of total value = **`$7,800/yr`** in 2026$ for single-family `[Property A]`; higher for older 3-unit coastal Outer Sunset salt-fog exposure `[Property B]` = **`$12,600/yr`** in 2026$, matching user's statement *"Regular maintenance and repair costs, not as much as the san francisco house"*).
3. **Real Estate Selling & Documentary Transfer Tax Costs:**
   - **`[Property A — Cupertino 95014]`:** Santa Clara County Documentary Transfer Tax is **`$1.10 per $1,000` (`0.11%`)** under `CA RTC § 11911` (City of Cupertino has no separate city transfer tax), plus ~`4.5%–5.0%` brokerage/title/escrow/prep = **`5.0%` total selling cost** (`5.11%` rounded in `$10` model). Source: [CA RTC § 11911](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=11911).
   - **`[Property B — San Francisco 94122]`:** SF imposes a steep tiered transfer tax: **`0.75%`** (`$1M–$5M`) and **`2.25%`** (`$5M–$10M`), making selling SF far more punitive in transaction taxes than selling Cupertino! Source: [SF Assessor-Recorder Transfer Tax](https://www.sfassessor.org/recorder-information/recording-document/transfer-tax).
