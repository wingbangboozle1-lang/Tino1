Questions to answer before deciding

Every question that must be answered before the live / rent / sell decision can be called reasonable: what the site assumes today, what is missing, and how to discover each missing item.

A decision can be drawn from the numbers already on this site, but it cannot be called reasoned until the six blocker questions are answered. This page is the single list: the questions that must be addressed first, the information already established and where it was read, and everything still missing with a suggested way to discover it. What is known is linked to the page it was read on; what is missing is left blank and flagged; nothing is inferred.
