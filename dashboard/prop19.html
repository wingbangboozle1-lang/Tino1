Worked calculation for Property A (model, base case)

Formula (BOE LTA 2026/026 definitions): excluded amount = factored base year value (FBYV) + adjusted $1 million; if full cash value (FMV) exceeds it, new taxable value = FBYV + (FMV − excluded amount). FBYV grows 2%/yr from the user-supplied 2025 value; the cap grows 2%/yr (assumption) from $1,044,586; FMV grows 4%/yr (assumption).

Property B (three units)

  • If the Transferor's principal residence is Property A, no part of Property B qualifies: all three units are reassessed to market value at death (rental/investment property — BOE Prop 19 page; LTA 2026/026 Q5).
  • If the Transferor instead lives in one unit of B, only that unit (about one-third of the value) can be excluded, and only if the Heir moves into that unit within one year; the two tenant-occupied units are reassessed regardless (Q5, Q15).
  • The Heir can claim the Homeowners' Exemption on only one home, so living in A and claiming B's unit (or vice-versa) is not possible.

Common pitfalls

  • Missing the one-year move-in / BOE-266 deadline — no exceptions (Q7).
  • Adding the Heir to title during life, or gifting the property: triggers change-in-ownership analysis now and forfeits the income-tax step-up.
  • Transferring a property into an LLC or to a non-child: the exclusion is personal to parent–child (and grandparent–grandchild with deceased middle generation).
  • Step-transaction: temporary occupancy or paper moves to qualify are disregarded (Q9).
