Reading the table. In 2045 a single Transferor can leave roughly $23,979,750 free of federal estate tax; after the two properties ($9,687,540) that leaves about $14,292,210 of room for cash and investments. Above that, each extra $1,000,000 costs $400,000 of estate tax — which is where lifetime annual-exclusion gifts ($19,000 per donee in 2026), charitable planning, or spousal portability matter. Inherited cash and securities also receive a stepped-up basis.
