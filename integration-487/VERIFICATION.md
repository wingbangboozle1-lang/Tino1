# Source and calculation verification

Review date: 2026-11-11 UTC. The official materials below were retrieved and read for the specific propositions listed. They are not sources for property-specific amounts: **all property and estate scenarios are synthetic**. External pages, laws, and links can change; this is a dated review, not a guarantee of future availability or legal advice.

## Property, trusts, and reporting

- [BOE Proposition 19 fact sheet](https://www.boe.ca.gov/pdf/pub801.pdf): qualifying family-home relationship, transferor and transferee principal residence, the one-year occupancy/exemption framework, claim timing, possible prospective relief, and the factored-value-plus-excess example. Eligibility was not inferred for any actual family.
- [BOE letter 2025/009](https://www.boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/pdf/lta25009.pdf): the family-home allowance for transfers from 2025-02-16 through 2027-02-15, rounded for display to $1,044,590. Future values are unknown; smooth 2.5% modeling is not the statutory biennial index calculation.
- [BOE assessment basics](https://www.boe.ca.gov/pdf/pub800-10.pdf): change in ownership, the 2% ceiling on the factored base, January 1 decline-in-value comparison, and recovery above 2% up to the factored ceiling. The model's min-of-market-and-factored shortcut omits assessor procedures and supplemental bills.
- [BOE homeowners’ exemption](https://www.boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/homeowners_exemption.htm): a qualifying principal residence can receive a $7,000 reduction in taxable value, not a credit of that size.
- [San Francisco assessor assessment roll](https://www.sf.gov/news-assessor-recorder-joaquin-torres-releases-2026-2027-assessment-roll): assessment growth, change in ownership, construction and decline-in-value context. A different property's transfer is not by itself a change in ownership of the separately owned property. The latter remains on its own track in the model.
- [San Francisco secured property taxes](https://sftreasurer.org/property/secured-property-taxes): official fiscal 2026–27 secured rate is 1.18162182%. The model instead uses an explicitly hypothetical 1.18%, plus invented direct charges; neither is an actual bill.
- [San Francisco Rent Board](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit): 1.6% allowable increase for the published March 2026–February 2027 period, subject to covered-unit rules; the November 9, 2026 notice identifies a pending, not enacted, proposal. Coverage and future rent ceilings are unverified.
- [Santa Clara County recorder](https://clerkrecorder.santaclaracounty.gov/recording-documents/recording-real-estate): the cited recording and county documentary-transfer-tax framework, exemptions and separate city taxes. The model's 5% all-in selling cost is invented, not a calculated tax quote.
- [California Probate Code §16061.7](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=16061.7.&lawCode=PROB): generally 60-day notice after specified events, with rules for later discovery, vacancy and exceptions.
- [California Revenue and Taxation Code §480](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=480.&lawCode=RTC): death transfers through trusts outside probate generally require the change-of-ownership statement within 150 days; probate uses inventory/appraisal timing.

## Federal and California tax

- [IRS 2027 inflation announcement](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-releases-tax-inflation-adjustments-for-tax-year-2027-taxpayers-continue-to-benefit-from-the-working-families-tax-cuts): $15,000,000 and $15,480,000 basic exclusions and $19,000 and $20,000 annual gift exclusions for 2026 and 2027 respectively. The 2028-onward model is hypothetical.
- [IRS Form 706 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i706): estate filing test includes gross estate, adjusted taxable gifts and specific exemption; portability may motivate a return below a size-based threshold; filing and payment dates/extensions are separate. The synthetic estate-tax calculation is a deliberately rough proxy, not Form 706.
- [IRS Rev. Proc. 2022-32](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-22-32.pdf): the simplified fifth-anniversary late portability procedure has defined eligibility conditions and is not available to every estate.
- [IRS Rev. Rul. 2023-2](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rr-23-02.pdf) and [IRS Publication 551](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p551): basis adjustment depends on acquisition, estate inclusion and trust facts; the specific ruling disallows an adjustment under its stated excluded grantor-trust facts. Full basis and 50% basis are scenarios, not findings about the actual trust.
- [IRS Publication 523](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p523): the home-sale exclusion requires ownership/use/lookback conditions, with rental and depreciation limitations. The model assumes single-filer eligibility after two complete years only as a scenario.
- [IRS Publication 527](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p527): residential rental building depreciation generally uses 27.5 years under GDS and land is not depreciable; the real mid-month convention is simplified in this model. Rental expenses and income are fact-specific.
- [IRS NIIT guidance](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc559): 3.8% applies to the lesser of qualifying net investment income or relevant modified-income excess; trust and individual thresholds differ. It is not applied automatically to all gains in this simplified blended-rate model.
- [California FTB capital gains](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/personal/income-types/capital-gains-and-losses.html): California generally treats capital gains as ordinary income. The assumed 30% / 35% combined effective rates are invented, not state or federal statutory rates.
- [California Controller estate tax](https://www.sco.ca.gov/ardtax_estate_tax.html): no California estate return requirement for deaths on or after 2005; the Controller discusses inheritance tax for earlier deaths.

## Economics, insurance, investments, and proposals

- [BLS September 2026 CPI](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_10142026.htm): CPI-U increased 3.7% in the 12 months to September 2026. [Historical annual-average CPI](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/tables/supplemental-files/historical-cpi-u-202402.pdf): selected observations range from negative to double-digit inflation. Neither is a future projection.
- [FHFA Q2 2026 house-price report](https://www.fhfa.gov/document/d/hpi/fhfa-house-price-index-report-2026q2): U.S. purchase-only index +2.1% and California -0.2% year over year. An index does not appraise an individual property.
- [California insurance guide](https://www.insurance.ca.gov/01-consumers/105-type/95-guides/03-res/res-ins-guide.cfm): premiums, terms and exclusions should be compared. No actual policy quote or coverage is verified.
- [CPUC net-metering guide](https://www.cpuc.ca.gov/industries-and-topics/electrical-energy/demand-side-management/customer-generation/net-energy-metering-and-net-billing): tariffs depend on interconnection and service circumstances; eligible legacy tariffs generally have a 20-year transition. No actual solar tariff, credit or payment is assumed.
- [FDIC deposit coverage](https://www.fdic.gov/resources/deposit-insurance/understanding-deposit-insurance): standard coverage is $250,000 per depositor, insured bank and ownership category, with aggregation; investment funds are not FDIC-insured deposits.
- [TreasuryDirect security types](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/indiv/help/treasurydirect-help/user-guide/201-210/): Treasury interest is generally federally taxable and exempt from state/local income tax. Market price and liquidity may vary.
- [SEC mutual funds and ETFs](https://www.sec.gov/investor/pubs/sec-guide-to-mutual-funds.pdf): diversification, investment losses, fees and taxes require attention; the modeled 4% after-tax return is a proxy, not a guaranteed product return.
- [Congress S.587](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/587/all-info): introduced and referred, not enacted as reviewed. [California SB 288](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billStatusClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260SB288): held in committee in August 2026, not enacted as reviewed. Proposed San Francisco rent legislation is identified as not enacted in the Rent Board notice above.
