Step 7
Decision fork (Month 3–6)		Choose path: (a) Sell Property A from trust or after distribution, (b) Distribute & rent out, or (c) Move in as primary residence within 12 months of date of death.CA R&TC § 63.2 ↗ · BOE LTA 2026/026 ↗
Step 8
Within 9 months of death		File Federal Form 706 if gross estate + adjusted taxable gifts exceed the year-of-death exclusion ($15,000,000 in 2026; $15,480,000 in 2027; ~$24,143,520 projected in 2045) OR to elect spousal DSUE portability (Form 4768 grants 6-month filing extension).IRS Form 706 Instructions (07/2026) ↗ · IRS Estate Tax ↗

Within 30 days after Form 706		If Form 706 is required under IRC § 6018, file Form 8971 and furnish Schedule A consistent-basis statements to beneficiaries.IRS Form 706 Instructions (07/2026) ↗ · IRS Estate & Gift What's New ↗
Step 10
California & local estate return		No California estate or inheritance tax return is required for deaths on or after January 1, 2005 ($0 CA & $0 local death tax).CA State Controller Estate Tax ↗ · Santa Clara Clerk-Recorder DTT ↗
Step 11
If living in Property A (within 1 year)		Move into Property A within 12 months of death, file Form BOE-266 (Homeowners' Exemption) within 1 year, and file Form BOE-19-P (Prop 19 exclusion) within 3 years; terminate HOX on SF Unit 3 before Dec 10.CA R&TC § 63.2 ↗ · BOE Pub. 800-1 ↗

If renting Property A		Replace homeowners policy with a landlord / dwelling-fire policy before tenant move-in and include mandatory written AB 1482 single-family exemption language in the lease.CDI Residential Insurance Guide (2026) ↗ · CA DOJ Tenant Protection Act ↗
Step 13

Step 14
Escrow tax-withholding guard on sale		Complete California FTB Form 593 Part VI (Alternative Withholding Calculation) in escrow so 3.33% of gross sale price is NOT withheld when stepped-up gain is $0.FTB Form 593 Instructions ↗ · IRS Inherited Property Basis FAQ ↗
Step 15
April 15 following year of death/sale		File decedent's final Form 1040/540, trust Form 1041 + Schedule K-1 (if trust income > $600), and report Property A sale on Form 8949 / Schedule D showing stepped-up basis.IRS Form 1041 Filing Guide ↗ · IRS Topic 409 (Capital Gains) ↗
