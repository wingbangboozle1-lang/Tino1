Deadlines as a timeline

When each step falls, counted from the date of death. Confirm every date with the attorney and the county office.

Deadlines are the ones on the Process page, arranged in time order. Each row links to the statute, form or agency page it comes from. Counting starts from the date of death.

  1. Now, while the Transferor is alive
    Ask the estate attorney which trust holds Property A and whether it is in the gross estate (Q01). Keep the homeowners' exemption on file. Rev. Rul. 2023-2 · BOE homeowners' exemption
  2. Date of death (day 0)
    Record the date. Order a written appraisal as of that date: it sets the basis and the change-in-ownership value. IRS inherited-property FAQ
  3. Within 60 days
    The successor trustee notifies every beneficiary and heir. Prob. Code 16061.7
  4. Within 150 days
    File the change-in-ownership statement for Property A with the Santa Clara County Assessor. R&TC 480
  5. Within 9 months
    File Form 706 if the estate is above the filing threshold or to elect portability. Form 4768 adds six months. Form 706 instructions · IRS estate FAQ
  6. Within 1 year (only if moving in)
    Become the principal residence and file the homeowners' exemption (BOE-266) before the exclusion claim. R&TC 63.2 · BOE Pub. 800-1
  7. Within 3 years (only if moving in)
    File the parent-child exclusion claim (BOE-19-P) with the Assessor, before any transfer to a third party. BOE Prop 19 · Santa Clara Assessor
  8. February 2027 (rule change date)
    The Proposition 19 allowance of $1,044,586 applies until 2027-02-15; the next adjustment takes effect on 2027-02-16. Adjustments are made every second year. BOE LTA 2025/009
  9. Tax year of any sale
    Report the sale on Form 8949 and Schedule D using the date-of-death basis. IRS inherited-property FAQ · IRS Topic 409
  10. Five years after death
    Last date for a late portability election under the IRS relief procedure. IRS estate FAQ

Built 2026-11-13 from final/ (model, generator and checker). Earlier versions are superseded. Property A = Santa Clara County home ([ZIP A]). Property B = San Francisco three-unit home ([ZIP B]). People are placeholders: "the Transferor" (current owner of Property A) and "the Inheritor" (already owns Property B). Owner-supplied and modelled dollar figures are rounded to the nearest $10.
