Deadline timeline

Every filing clock in the inheritance process, in order, with the official page for each.

Dates are relative to the date of death (D). The trust does not change the Prop. 19 steps. Items marked "only if moving in" do not apply to a sale or rent-out.

  1. NowBefore any death
    Have the estate attorney confirm the trust type and whether Property A will be inside the gross estate. Keep Property A as the Transferor's principal residence with the homeowners' exemption on file.

    Rev. Rul. 2023-2 · BOE Prop 19 · BOE homeowners' exemption flag Q01

  2. Date of death (D)Day 0
    Record the date. Get a written appraisal of Property A as of that date; it sets the new basis.

    BOE Rule 462.260 · IRS inherited-property FAQ

  3. D + 60 daysTrustee notice
    If the trust was revocable: the successor trustee serves the notification on every beneficiary and heir (120-day contest warning).

    Prob. Code 16061.7

  4. D + 150 daysChange in ownership
    File the change-in-ownership statement for Property A with the Santa Clara County Assessor.

    R&TC 480

  5. D + 9 monthsFederal return
    File Form 706 if required or to elect portability. Form 4768 gives six more months.

    IRS estate FAQ · Form 706 instructions flag Q02

  6. D + 1 year (only if moving in)Principal residence
    Make Property A the Inheritor's principal residence and file BOE-266 before the Prop. 19 claim.

    R&TC 63.2 · BOE LTA 2026/026 · BOE Pub. 800-1

  7. D + 3 years (only if moving in)Prop. 19 claim
    File BOE-19-P with the Assessor. Late claims get prospective relief only.

    BOE Prop 19 · Santa Clara Assessor · BOE Pub. 800-1 flag S03

  8. After the Assessor actsSupplemental bill
    Expect a supplemental tax bill for Property A covering the period from the date of death.

    SF Treasurer flag U06

  9. Each January 1 (lien date)Homeowners' exemption
    The owner-occupied $7,000 exemption applies on the January 1 lien date; file by February 15 for the full exemption.

    BOE homeowners' exemption

  10. Tax year of any saleSale reporting
    Report the sale on Form 8949 and Schedule D using the date-of-death value as basis.

    IRS inherited-property FAQ · IRS Topic 409

  11. Every year, if rentedRent reporting
    Report rent and expenses; depreciate the building over 27.5 years; follow the rent-cap notices.

    IRS Pub. 527 · CA Attorney General
