How to get the official insurance figure in five minutes

  1. Open the 2026 Homeowners Insurance comparison tool of the California Department of Insurance.
  2. Choose Homeowners, then the county, then the ZIP code (95014, then 94122), a coverage amount near the rebuilding cost and the age of the home.
  3. Read the range of annual premiums across insurers and compare it with the $1,500 estimate used here.
  4. For Property B ask an agent for a landlord (dwelling fire) quote for a three-unit building; the survey has no such scenario.
