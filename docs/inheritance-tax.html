Tax Law — every rule that touches this inheritance

No California estate or inheritance tax. One federal estate tax with a high exemption. One property-tax system (Prop 13) with one inheritance exception (Prop 19). One basis rule (step-up) that makes the sell-vs-hold math work. All numbers verified, all sources linked per row.
