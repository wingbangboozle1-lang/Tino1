Why step-up dominates: Suppose parents bought Cupertino decades ago for $400k and it’s $3.1M at death. Without step-up you’d face tax on $2.7M gain → ~$405k federal (15%) + $108k NIIT + ~$360k CA = ~$873k combined. With step-up, that $2.7M is erased. You only pay on appreciation after death. This is the single strongest argument to not sell before death and to sell promptly after if you don’t want the house.
