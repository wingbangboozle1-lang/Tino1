Houses total $4,900,000 (2026). Federal tax only if total estate (houses + cash + investments) exceeds $15M/person. OBBBA (P.L. 119-21 §70106) made $15M permanent (no sunset), indexed from 2027. Estate tax is paid by the estate (Form 706), not the heir. File Form 706 for portability even if no tax due.

5. Pending law changes to watch

  • Prop 19 repeal initiative (2026 ballot attempt): failed to qualify (needed 874,641 signatures) — current Prop 19 rules stand. Ballotpedia
  • Split-roll / Prop 13 reform: rumored for 2026 ballot (commercial property); HJTA prioritizing Prop 13 defense. No enacted change as of Nov 2026 — monitor. HJTA
  • Prop 19 cap: re-indexed biennially (FHFA HPI); next update Feb 2027. BOE
  • Estate exemption: permanent $15M (OBBBA); future Congress could change. IRS
