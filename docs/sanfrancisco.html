Decision Implication — Why SF Should Usually Stay

Keep scenario

  • ✔ Free housing: live in 3rd unit, zero rent expense, retain $85,800 gross
  • ✔ Rent-control hedge: existing tenants may be below market — protects against vacancy
  • ✔ Lower tax sensitivity: even after inheritance, tax is $8,920 (live-in) vs Cupertino’s $24,250
  • ✔ Diversification anchor: if you sell Cupertino and reinvest, SF gives you real-estate exposure + tax-advantaged depreciation (rental portion) while you stay liquid

Sell scenario — when it makes sense

  • Sell only if: (a) building needs $100k+ seismic/structural, or (b) you want to consolidate in equities, or (c) you move permanently to Cupertino/South Bay and don’t want to be a SF landlord.
  • Proceeds if sold at $1.8M → net ~$1,701,000 after 5.5% costs; stepped-up basis → ~$0 cap gains if sold promptly.
  • Consider 1031 exchange into a turnkey single-tenant or DST if you want to stay in real estate but simplify management. (Note: CA 1031 taxes gains at ordinary rates up to 13.3% if not deferred.)
