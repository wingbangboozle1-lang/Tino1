💰 Live / Rent / Sell — Cupertino Decision Head-to-Head

Assumes inheritance in 2040 at market values from Projections (Cupertino $5,741,030; SF $3,034,140). SF triplex kept as live-in anchor (3rd unit occupied, $85,800 gross base in 2026 dollars, escalated at 3%/yr). All numbers rounded to $10. After-tax where noted.

Framework: We compare Cupertino only — because the SF house is strongly “keep” in all scenarios (see SF page). You can absolutely keep both; the question is what you do with Cupertino. Three mutually exclusive Cupertino paths: A) Live in it (Prop 19 partial), B) Rent it (full tax, landlord), C) Sell it within 12 months of death (stepped-up basis → ~$0 cap gains) and reinvest net proceeds.

Bottom Line — Two Paths, One Recommendation

⭐ Financial optimum — Maximize profit, minimize cost, reduce uncertainty

Keep SF Outer Sunset (live in 3rd unit), sell Cupertino within 12 months of inheritance, reinvest net $5.43M (2040 value) into broad market.

  • ✔ Eliminates $55–105k/yr property tax carry on Cupertino (growing 2%/yr forever)
  • ✔ Harvests stepped-up basis — ~$0 cap gains on $2.6M+ of lifetime appreciation
  • ✔ Converts concentrated, illiquid, high-tax single asset into diversified liquid portfolio that historically compounds 2–3% faster after-tax
  • ✔ Preserves SF income ($85k+ gross) + free housing + depreciation benefits
  • ✔ Reduces complexity: 1 county, 1 rent board, 1 tax bill vs 2
  • ✔ Surpasses renting Cupertino by ~$1M in 10 yr, ~$4.8M in 20 yr (see table)

If you love Cupertino schools/community, the lifestyle premium is ~$27k/yr net (after avoided rent) in 2040$ — that’s the price. Pay it consciously if worth it.

Legacy optimum — If you deeply want to live in Cupertino

Move in within 1 year, file BOE-19-P + HOX immediately, budget $68,770/yr carry (2040$) growing 2–3%, and keep SF as rental.

  • ✔ Secures $12,330/yr Prop 19 saving (vs renting) — file day 1
  • ✔ Elite Cupertino schools; larger lot; South Bay networks; solar surplus
  • ✔ Both houses appreciate; you capture full 4.5% Cupertino CAGR (vs 6.5% market — so opportunity cost is ~2%/yr)
  • ✔ Plan EQ insurance ($2,000–$4,000/yr extra) and eventual capital needs (roof $25k–45k, HVAC $12k–20k, etc.) — see Cupertino costs
  • ⚠️ Consider renting SF triplex fully (move out of SF) vs keeping 2 units rented + living in Cupertino — you lose free housing but gain SF rent on 3rd unit (+~$38k/yr in 2040$)

Renting Cupertino as a non-owner-occupied landlord is almost never optimal — negative after-tax carry plus active management. Sell instead if not living there.

The irrevocable question to answer now (2026), not in 2040: Will an heir actually live in Cupertino for 5+ years after death? If answer is “probably not,” pre-commit to the sell path, pre-select an agent/estate attorney, and tell siblings/trustee — so the 1-year clock doesn’t expire while you debate.
