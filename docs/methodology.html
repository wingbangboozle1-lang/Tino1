Methodology, parameters and privacy

Transparent inputs, formulas and rules so every number can be reproduced.

How the numbers are produced

  1. data/*.csv hold every verified fact with its official URL and a status.
  2. analysis/model.py projects values, assessed values, taxes and costs year by year (2026–2060), runs the six scenarios and the 27-case sensitivity grid, and writes data/projections/*.csv, data/scenarios.csv and docs/data/model_output.json.
  3. analysis/build_site.py renders this site from those files. Re-run both scripts after changing any parameter.

Core formulas

  • Assessed value (no change in ownership): AVt = AVt−1 × 1.02 (Cal. Const. art. XIII A §2(b) maximum).
  • Prop 19 at death: excluded amount = FBYV + cap; new taxable value = FBYV if FMV ≤ excluded amount, else FBYV + (FMV − excluded amount); without the exclusion, new taxable value = FMV.
  • Property tax = (AV − $7,000 if owner-occupied) × rate + direct charges (grown 2%/yr).
  • Net rent = gross rent × (1 − vacancy − management); income tax = max(net rent − costs − depreciation, 0) × 37.1%.
  • Sale proceeds = price − 5% selling costs − transfer tax − 37.1% × (gain − §121 exclusion if applicable) − 41.1% × accumulated depreciation; basis = value at death (§1014).
  • Investment account: balancet = balancet−1 × (1 + r) + net casht; at the horizon, gains above contributions are taxed at 30%.
  • Estate tax = 40% × max(gross estate − exclusion × (2 if portability), 0); exclusion = $15,000,000 × 1.025(year − 2026).

Privacy rules applied

  • No names, street addresses, parcel numbers, account numbers or dates of birth anywhere in the repository or site.
  • Properties identified by ZIP code only; people as Transferor / Heir.
  • Owner-supplied year-by-year assessment histories are not published (they are searchable identifiers); only a rounded anchor and a ratio are kept.
  • All amounts rounded to the nearest $10.

Limitations

  • Deterministic model: no probability distributions; see the sensitivity grid for ranges.
  • Heir's personal tax situation, filing status, other income and residence are assumed (high-income single California resident).
  • Rent-control status of Property B and the Transferor's principal residence are assumed (flagged).
  • Not legal, tax or investment advice.
