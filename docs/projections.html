Projections 2026–2050 — what the houses and taxes do by year

Method: market value grows at 3.5%/yr (mid-point of C.A.R. 2026 forecast 3.6% statewide, 2–6% Bay Area). Assessed value grows at Prop 13 2%/yr until death, then resets per Prop 19 (cap $1,044,586 for Feb 16 2025–Feb 15 2027, adjusted biennially — we model cap inflating at 2%/yr). Property tax = assessed × composite rate (Cupertino ~1.33% billed / ~1.16% ad-valorem baseline; SF ~2.1% effective / 1.1827% base). Insurance +5%/yr, water/electric +3%, maintenance +3%, rent +3%. Inheritance illustrated at 2045 (midpoint); 2040 and 2050 sensitivities provided.
