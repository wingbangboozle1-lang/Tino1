Projections

Forty-five years of projected market values, assessed values, property tax and carrying costs for both properties. Inheritance modeled at 2045, the midpoint of the stated 2040–2050 window. Every projection resting on a model assumption is marked Projected; verified statutory inputs are marked separately on Sources.

How to read these numbers. Market values and assessed values are two different things in California, and the gap between them is the analysis. Between reassessment events the assessed value can rise no more than 2% a year no matter what the market does. A property worth $17.41M in 2070 may be assessed at only $8.18M — that gap is Proposition 13, and it is what Proposition 19 puts at risk.
