Home-sale exclusion timingThe existing model subtracts the assumed home-sale exclusion even in its first modeled year of residence. Ordinarily the ownership and use tests require two years within the five-year period; exceptions and partial exclusions require their own fact-dependent assessment. Apply the rule only when modeled eligibility is established.IRS: ownership and use
No-step-up basisIn the no-step-up sensitivity, the model substitutes the property-tax assessed value for income-tax basis. That substitution is not verified. Tax basis depends on acquisition and later adjustments; leave it unknown instead of deriving it from an assessment.IRS Publication 530
Investment income taxThe model applies a fixed net investment income tax rate without testing modified adjusted gross income or the relevant threshold, and without determining whether the income is subject to the tax. Label this a sensitivity assumption, not a determination of liability.IRS Topic 559
Rent proxyHUD small-area fair-market rent is a program benchmark for an area and unit size, not confirmation of the actual rent of a particular property. An inferred rent for an unrented unit is likewise unverified. Avoid representing either as known property income.HUD SAFMR evaluation
Potential loss on saleA difference between sale proceeds and basis is not necessarily a deductible loss: the IRS says a loss on personal-use property is not deductible. The use of the inherited property and holding period need fact-specific review.IRS Topic 409
Estate roomSubtracting the house alone from a future projected exemption does not establish remaining estate-tax capacity. Other includible property, lifetime taxable gifts, deductions, prior spouse exclusion and future law can matter. The actual amount is unknown.IRS Form 706 instructions
Best-plan headlineDo not treat the model ranking as a verified personalized recommendation: it depends on assumed rent, taxes, basis, insurance, returns, transaction timing and other missing facts.
