REVIEW BEFORE ACTION / 08

Suggestions & open questions

Review these options and evidence gaps before making a property or investment decision. Nothing here implements a change.

First priority: contain exposure

The public repository and its history may contain household information even when Pages excludes it. Rounding is not anonymization. Review access and GitHub sensitive-data removal guidance; caches, forks and third-party copies may persist. This page does not publish private documents or request uploads.
