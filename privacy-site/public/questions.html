DECISION READINESS / 08

Questions before deciding

A checklist for discovering missing information without publishing private records.

Answer in order

The gates below are sequential: a later gate cannot be answered honestly while an earlier one is open. Each row names the evidence that closes it and the page that explains the rule. Nothing here asks you to submit a document to this site.

Decision gates in order · what must be true before a decision is reasonable
GateQuestion to settleWhat is neededHow to obtain it privatelyWhy it comes hereOfficial context
Gate 1Is the inherited home legally and practically transferable to the Inheritor?Trust terms, current title, debts, any conditions or co-beneficiaries, and who has authority to act.Trust document, amendments, deed and trustee correspondence, read privately by counsel.Unblocks everything else: no path can be evaluated until ownership is established.IRS · Revenue Ruling 2023-2 ↗
Gate 2Are the family-home conditions actually met?Eligible relationship, transferor principal residence, transferee move-in within one year, and the exemption claim filed first.Counsel and the county assessor review facts against BOE Publication 800-1 and the claim forms.Decides whether the old assessment can continue or the home is reassessed at market value; this is the largest single cost lever.BOE · Publication 800-1 (Prop 19) ↗
Gate 3What is the value and basis on the transfer date?Date-of-death fair market value, alternate-valuation eligibility, adjusted basis of the already-owned home, and any basis-consistency reporting.A qualified appraisal plus acquisition and improvement records, reviewed by a tax preparer.Determines the gain if the home is sold and the depreciation if it is rented.IRS · Inherited property basis ↗
Gate 4Does the estate exceed the federal exclusion, and is there liquidity?Gross estate, prior taxable gifts, deductions, portability, and the exclusion in force at the transfer year.A confidential estate inventory and Form 706 analysis by estate counsel and a tax professional.An estate-tax or liquidity problem can force a sale regardless of which path is preferred.IRS · Estate tax ↗
Gate 5What would each path actually cash-flow?Verified achievable rent, vacancy, management, lawful unit status, insurance quote, water, electricity or solar true-up, repairs and capital reserve.Leases, a full year of bills, a tariff or solar agreement, inspection, and occupancy-specific insurance quotes, collected privately.Turns the rent and live-in paths from concepts into comparable numbers.CA DOI · Residential Insurance Guide (Form 401) ↗
Gate 6Which path wins on the same end date, after tax and after risk?After-tax liquidation wealth at one horizon, liquidity reserve, concentration, and tolerance for loss.The household’s own comparison using the evidence from Gates 1 to 5, reviewed with a fiduciary adviser.The only step that produces a decision; everything above is evidence gathering.SEC · Asset allocation and diversification ↗
Questions, current answers and suggested evidence · links supply context, not proof of household facts
QuestionWhat needs answeringCurrent answerHow to discoverOfficial context
Trust and titleDoes the trust transfer A to the intended beneficiary, and what rights or conditions apply?Unknown; a trust does not itself establish ownership, tax inclusion or basis.Counsel privately examines the complete trust, amendments and current title.IRS · Revenue Ruling 2023-2 ↗
Family-home exclusionAre the relationship, transferor residence and beneficiary occupancy requirements met?Unknown; moving in alone does not establish eligibility.Ask the county assessor and counsel to review facts and current BOE claims before the deadline.California BOE · Proposition 19 ↗
Values and taxesWhat are the current fair market values, factored bases, direct charges and transfer-date supplemental taxes?Unknown; no verified parcel-specific figures published.Obtain assessor records and a qualified appraisal privately; reconcile the tax bill and exemptions.California · RTC §51 ↗
Estate tax and liquidityWhat assets, debts, prior taxable gifts, deductions and portability are relevant?Unknown; neither the taxable estate nor remaining exemption is established.Estate counsel and a tax professional inventory assets and review prior returns and Form 706.IRS · Estate tax ↗
Income tax basisDoes A qualify for a basis adjustment, and what is B’s adjusted basis?Unknown; property-tax assessment is not income-tax basis.Review trust inclusion, acquisition and improvement records and obtain a date-of-death appraisal.IRS · Inherited property basis ↗
Rental economicsWhat rents are legal and achievable; what are vacancy, management, tax and capital costs?Unknown; existing rent is not a new benefit of inheritance.Privately review leases, local rules, inspections and independent property-manager estimates.IRS · Topic 414 ↗
Insurance and utilitiesWhat will actual coverage, water, solar true-up and other utilities cost in each use?Unknown; estimates or solar credits are not binding quotes or guaranteed profits.Obtain occupancy-specific policies and a full year of bills and tariff/solar agreements privately.California DOI · Residential insurance ↗
B housing and rentalsWhich space can be occupied or lawfully rented, and what changes if the Inheritor moves?Unknown; do not assume new rent or alter common income without evidence.Review permits, leases, occupancy and applicable local rules with counsel.California Legislature · Civil Code §1947.12 ↗
Investment and riskWhat time horizon, liquidity reserve, tax situation and tolerance for losses apply?Unknown; a return assumption is not a verified outcome.Compare after-tax diversified alternatives and cash reserves with a fiduciary adviser.SEC · Asset allocation and diversification ↗
Keep answers private

Use a secure channel with the trustee and licensed advisers. Do not upload deeds, bills, names, addresses, account balances or completed answers to this public site or repository.
