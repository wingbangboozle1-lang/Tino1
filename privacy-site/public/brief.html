Decision paths to compare

□ Live in A　 □ Rent A　 □ Sell A and reinvest

Compare on the same date and after tax. Include property tax, insurance, utilities, repairs/capital work, management/vacancy, transaction costs, liquidity and final liquidation taxes.

For reinvestment, compare immediate exposure with a 3-month-to-2-year Treasury parking/ladder path and a defined phased stock allocation. Show reinvestment risk at each maturity; staging does not guarantee protection from loss or better returns.

Why a dated record matters

A projection read today and a decision taken in 2045 are different things. Recording the choice, the date, the person who confirmed it and the document relied on is what lets a future reader — including a successor trustee or a later adviser — see why a path was taken instead of re-deriving it from one forecast. Leave every entry blank until it is actually decided; an invented entry is worse than an empty one.
