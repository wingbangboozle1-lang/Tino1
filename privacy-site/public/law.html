How much room remains?

The remaining household exclusion and cash that could pass without federal estate tax are unknown. Do not subtract only a house value. The estate computation includes includible assets, prior taxable gifts, deductions, credits and any valid portability. Filing threshold and actual liability are different. Crossing a threshold is not itself a tax penalty; late filing/payment and tax liability are separate issues.

No fabricated household cash threshold

No estate inventory, prior-gift history, citizenship/residency determination, trust inclusion analysis or portability election has been verified. No local estate-tax threshold is established in this audit; local property, recording and sale charges are separate and parcel/transaction dependent. Current California Controller guidance is not a guarantee about every local charge.
