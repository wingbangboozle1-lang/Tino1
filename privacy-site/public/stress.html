When the unexpected happens

Separate stress tests from the baseline. Historical episodes illustrate variability, not probabilities or predictions.

How to use these tests

Model shocks jointly as well as individually: for example a property decline together with higher repair costs and a stock market drop. Compare outcomes under different timing, including immediately before and after the transfer. Keep liquidity sufficient for taxes and essential repairs; no numerical reserve is verified here. Do not extrapolate national CPI or regional house indices into a household-specific forecast.
