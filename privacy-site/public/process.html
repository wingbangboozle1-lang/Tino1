PRACTICAL ROADMAP / 05

From trust to decision

The exact sequence depends on the trust, title, beneficiaries and the law at the time.

StepPhaseActionOfficial context
1Before the transferReview the trust, ownership, beneficiaries, powers, debts, prior gifts and likely estate inclusion with counsel.California Probate Code § 16061.7 ↗
2At deathObtain death certificates, secure the property, notify the trustee and insurer, and document the date and condition.California Probate Code § 16061.7 ↗
3Value assetsCommission a date-of-death appraisal and inventory all estate assets and liabilities; assess whether alternate valuation or exceptions apply.IRS · Form 706 instructions ↗
4Review basisDetermine the applicable income-tax basis; trust status alone does not guarantee a step-up.IRS · Revenue Ruling 2023-2 ↗
5Notify assessorFile the applicable change-in-ownership documents and ask the county about supplemental assessments and deadlines.Santa Clara County · assessor ↗
6If seeking family-home reliefCheck eligibility and occupy and file the homeowners’ or disabled veterans’ exemption within one year to seek relief from transfer date; file the parent-child claim within the applicable period.California BOE · parent–child transfers ↗
7Estate tax filingDetermine whether Form 706 is required or useful for portability. It is generally due nine months after death; a filing extension is available, but does not automatically extend payment.IRS · estate tax FAQ ↗
8Choose and implementReview net sale proceeds or rental economics, housing needs and portfolio risks; keep records of all decisions and expenditures.IRS · sale of home ↗
9Cash and retirement accountsConfirm ownership, named beneficiaries and account type; inherited retirement accounts may have separate distribution requirements.IRS · inherited IRAs ↗
