Compare the paths

A good decision compares after-tax wealth, cash flow, liquidity and housing needs on the same timeline.

Three choices, one consistent comparison

Working direction, not a winner

Preserving the already-owned home is a reasonable starting point to investigate; it is not automatically optimal. Selling the inherited home could reduce concentrated real-estate exposure, but no winner can be verified without the trust terms, basis, costs and risk preferences.

Comparison logic

For each path, calculate after-tax annual cash flow and ending wealth after a hypothetical sale on the same date. Include owner housing value or replacement housing cost, net rent, tax, insurance, utilities, maintenance, vacancy, sale costs and investment taxes. Avoid counting the same housing or rent benefit twice. Treat all starting values, rates and future outcomes as unverified until privately documented.
