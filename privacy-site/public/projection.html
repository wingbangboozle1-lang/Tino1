Synthetic annual illustration

Assume an unrelated fictional home is worth $1,000,000 in 2026 with $200,000 factored assessment. Home value grows 3%; inflation 2.5%; base assessment grows 2%. Transfer is assumed at the start of 2046 with a full-year tax simplification. The invented family-home allowance is held at $1,000,000, not the actual BOE allowance. Eligible live-in assessment = old factored base + max(0, market − old factored base − allowance). Rental reassessment = market. Thereafter factored bases grow 2%; taxable assessment is the lesser of factored base and market. Tax rate 1.2% is invented; no homeowner exemption, direct charges or prorated supplemental bill is included.

Real value = nominal value divided by 1.025 to the power of years since 2026. Amounts are computed unrounded, then displayed to $10. A 2% base adjustment is a scenario assumption within the cap, not a guaranteed increase.
