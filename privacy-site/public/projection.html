MODEL & METHOD / 03

Year-by-year outlook

A 50-year horizon needs explicit assumptions, not false precision. Dashes mean withheld or not independently verified.

No household forecast is published

Estimates, bills, quotes and personal inputs cannot be corroborated from official public sources without exposing the household. Likewise, future prices, rates and costs are not verifiable facts. They are intentionally blank.

Projection structure

In a private analysis, carry forward each year’s market value using that year’s assumed return, assessed value under the applicable rules, and each expense using its own escalation path. At the transfer date recalculate the assessment using the applicable eligibility and law. Include fixed charges separately from ad valorem tax. Calculate real dollars by dividing nominal amounts by cumulative inflation. None of these formulas verifies future outcomes.
