EXECUTIVE BRIEF / 01

A clearer path forward

A future trust inheritance. An already-owned home. Three paths, compared without exposing a household.

WORKING DIRECTION · CONDITIONAL

Preserve flexibility.
Compare before you commit.

Keep the already-owned home as the housing baseline. Evaluate selling the future inherited home and diversifying as the first financial benchmark—not an automatic winner. Renting must justify its after-tax yield and concentrated risk; moving in must justify its housing benefit and costs.

THE IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Property A
Future inheritance.

Property B is already owned. Inheriting A does not itself reset B’s assessment or income-tax basis. Keep B’s ongoing rental income in every path unless facts actually change.
