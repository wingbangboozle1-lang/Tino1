Public-site privacy has limits

This guide has no forms, scripts, analytics, external fonts or uploads. The repository itself is public and contains household-derived data outside this guide; Pages exclusions do not protect repository files. Your browser contacts source sites only when you follow their links; GitHub states that visitor IP addresses are logged for security purposes; see the GitHub source below. The repository remains a separate exposure surface. No private figures were imported into the new illustrative model.

Verification method

Official pages were opened and the relevant passages read on 12 November 2026. Legal facts are distinguished from workflow judgments, missing records and illustrative assumptions. Each data row links to official context; a link is not evidence that the source certifies a forecast, expense or recommendation. Broken candidate URLs were rejected, including the old BOE Prop13 URL and an incorrect Investor.gov path. No live property searches were used.

Delivery and completeness

This edition supplies an executive brief, decision gates, current tax-law findings, a trust roadmap, operating-cost and history verification registers, annual synthetic calculations and black-swan illustrations. It deliberately does not claim a verified household forecast, exact future tax, binding insurance price, total remaining estate allowance, exhaustive pending-law search or guaranteed best investment. Review flags do not block publication; failures of the build or publication allowlist do.
