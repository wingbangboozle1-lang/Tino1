Insurance research result

The California Department of Insurance provides premium and coverage comparison tools and recommends shopping policies. Those tools do not establish a binding primary-home or mixed-use landlord premium without building, coverage, claims and occupancy details. No official quote was obtained, so none is filled in. FAIR Plan coverage is limited; separate difference-in-conditions coverage may be needed. Do not budget only a generic online average.

From gross rent to cash flow

Collected rent − tax − insurance − owner utilities − repairs − management − capital cash outlays − debt service − income taxes. Taxable rental profit differs because depreciation is noncash, principal repayment is not generally deductible rental expense, and improvements may be capitalized. Allocate mixed-use costs correctly; check passive-loss restrictions.
