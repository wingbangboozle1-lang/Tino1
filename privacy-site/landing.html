Executive summary · decision gates

What would change the answer?

Property A is the future inheritance; Property B is already owned. Compare only A’s incremental effect, keeping B’s existing income and housing use constant unless a documented change is intended.

Ownership and eligibilityConfirm trust, title, estate inclusion, relationship and occupancy facts. Validate Proposition 19 eligibility and filing deadlines before counting on assessment relief.
Tax and liquidityObtain a date-of-death valuation and basis advice; verify debt, selling and recording costs, inheritance-year sale tax, estate size, prior gifts and portability.
Real economics and goalsGet independent sale and rent evidence, check leases and local rules, price insurance/utilities, build a five-year capital plan, and write the investment-risk and liquidity policy.

Keep evidence privately with qualified advisers. Open the questions and evidence checklist or review suggestions before implementation.
