Decision log: what was decided, on what date, and why

Every choice this analysis made, with its date and its reason, so that a reader in 2045 can see what was settled and on what evidence.

Decisions taken by the analysis

Why this page exists. A site that publishes numbers without publishing its decisions gives a reader no way to tell an assumption from a choice. Every row below is dated, says what was decided, says why, and links the official page or the model output it rests on. Where the decision was the analyst's and not the owner's, it says so.
