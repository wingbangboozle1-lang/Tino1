Glossary: terms used on this site

Every term that matters for the decision, in plain language, with the official page it comes from.

Glossary: 31 terms, each with the official page it comes from
TermWhat it meansWhere it matters on this siteOfficial basis
Basis (stepped-up basis)The starting value used to measure taxable gain on a sale. For inherited property it is generally the fair market value on the date of death.Sell and reinvest; the basis reset (question Q01)IRS inherited-property FAQ
Gross estateEverything the person owned at death that counts toward the federal estate tax. Whether the home is inside it decides the basis reset.Question Q01; the process stepsIRS estate tax
Federal basic exclusion amountThe amount each person can pass without federal estate tax: $15,000,000 for 2026 and $15,480,000 for 2027.Cash and estate tax pageIRS what's new · Rev. Proc. 2026-35
PortabilityA surviving spouse can use a deceased spouse's unused exclusion, but only if a timely estate tax return is filed.Question Q02IRS estate FAQ
Form 706 and Form 4768The federal estate tax return is due nine months after death. Form 4768 gives six more months.Process step 8Form 706 instructions · IRS estate FAQ
Proposition 13 assessed valueThe taxable value for property tax. It starts from the last change in ownership and rises by no more than 2% a year (or inflation, if lower).Both propertiesR&TC 51
Change in ownershipA transfer that triggers reassessment. For a revocable trust the change-in-ownership date is the date of death.Process steps 4 and 7BOE Rule 462.260
Proposition 19 parent-child exclusionLets a child keep most of the parent's assessed value, only if the child makes the home a principal residence and files the claim on time.Lived-in path (Property A)BOE Prop 19
Base year value (taxable value)The assessed value carried into the new ownership before any Proposition 19 adjustment.Lived-in path (Property A)BOE LTA 2026/026
Value limit ($1,044,586)The amount above the base year value that Proposition 19 still excludes for transfers from 16 Feb 2025 to 15 Feb 2027. It is adjusted every two years.Lived-in path (Property A)BOE LTA 2025/009
Homeowners' exemptionThe reduction of $7,000 in assessed value for an owner-occupied principal residence.Process steps 2 and 10BOE homeowners' exemption
Principal residenceThe home where the owner lives most of the time. Several rules (exclusion, exemption, Section 121) depend on it.Question Q15R&TC 63.2
Section 121 exclusionUp to $250,000 of gain ($500,000 for joint filers) is excluded when the home was the owner's residence for two of the five years before sale.Live-then-sell mixed pathIRS Topic 701
Depreciation recaptureDepreciation claimed on rented property is taxed when the property is sold, at up to 25% federal.Rent-then-sell mixed path; process step 14IRS Topic 409 · IRS Pub. 527
Net investment income taxA 3.8% tax on certain investment income, including rent and gains, above income thresholds.Every choice that earns incomeIRS Topic 559
Capital gains rates (0%, 15%, 20%)Federal tax rates on long-term gains, set by taxable income.Sale of the home and the stock portfolioIRS Topic 409
Form 1041The income tax return for a trust or estate with gross income above $600 in a year.Process step 6IRS estate income tax
Form 8971The basis report that must match when estate tax is due and the property is later sold.Process step 13IRS inherited-property FAQ
Revocable and irrevocable trust (Rev. Rul. 2023-2)Whether trust assets are in the gross estate decides whether they get the basis reset. Assets of an irrevocable grantor trust outside the estate get no reset.Question Q01Rev. Rul. 2023-2
Trust notice (Probate Code 16061.7)The successor trustee must notify beneficiaries and heirs within 60 days of death, which starts the contest period.Process step 5Prob. Code 16061.7
Net metering (solar credits)The rules under which a solar system exports power and the utility credits it. Current tariffs decide how long a credit lasts.Property A electricity lineCPUC net metering
San Francisco rent controlAnnual increases on covered units are capped by the Rent Board at a share of inflation.Property B rent growthSF rent increases
Statewide rent cap (Tenant Protection Act)A statewide cap on annual rent increases for covered units, with exemptions.Rent-it-out choice (Property A)CA Attorney General
Consumer Price Index (CPI)The official measure of consumer inflation used for every path.Every pathBLS CPI (FRED)
FHFA house price indexThe official home-price index used for the historical replays of the Bay Area.Black swan pageFHFA index, San Jose
Treasury par yield and 3-month billThe yield on government securities. The parked proceeds earn a rate close to these yields.Phased entry; what-if parking yieldTreasury yields, 13 Nov 2026
Dollar-cost averagingInvesting equal amounts at regular intervals, so that purchases are spread over time.Phase-in after the park periodSEC Investor.gov: dollar-cost averaging
Phased entry (model term)Parking the proceeds for 24 or 36 months in short Treasuries, then buying stocks in equal monthly steps over 12 months.Black swan page; what-if pageTreasury yields, 13 Nov 2026 · SEC Investor.gov: dollar-cost averaging
Mixed path (model term)Living in, or renting, Property A for a fixed number of years and then selling it.Decision page; what-if pageIRS Topic 701 · BOE Prop 19
Black swan path (model term)A path in which inflation, home prices and stocks move in bursts, busts and long flat stretches instead of a constant rate. A stress test, not a forecast.Black swan pageBLS CPI (FRED)
Reinvestment risk (model term)The risk that sale proceeds are invested at a bad time, or earn less than assumed. Shown beside the headline and on the what-if page.Front page; what-if pageTreasury yields, 13 Nov 2026
