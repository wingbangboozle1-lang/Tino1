Glossary

Plain-language definitions of the terms used on this site, with the official page each meaning comes from. Where a term has no official definition, it is labelled as the site's own usage.

Glossary
TermPlain-language meaningOfficial basis
Assessed valueThe value the county Assessor places on a home for property-tax purposes. Under Proposition 13 it starts at purchase price and can rise at most 2% a year unless ownership changes.BOE Proposition 13 · R&TC 51
Market valueThe price a willing buyer and seller would agree on in an open sale. Owner-supplied estimates on this site are rounded to the nearest $10 and are not appraisals.no official ZIP-level figure published - owner estimate flag P03 BOE Proposition 13
BasisGenerally what a taxpayer paid for an asset, adjusted for improvements and depreciation. Gain on a sale is sale price minus basis.IRS Topic 409 - Capital gains and losses
Basis step-up (stepped-up basis)When an asset is included in a taxable estate at death, its basis is reset to its market value at the date of death. A prompt sale then has little or no taxable gain.IRS inherited-property FAQ · Rev. Rul. 2023-2
Gross estateEverything a person owned or had an interest in at death, used to figure federal estate tax. Whether Property A is inside it decides whether the basis resets.IRS estate tax
Form 706The federal estate-tax return, due within 9 months of death (6-month extension via Form 4768), when the gross estate is above the filing threshold.Form 706 instructions
PortabilityA surviving spouse can use a deceased spouse's unused estate-tax exclusion, but only if Form 706 is filed on time.IRS estate FAQ
Proposition 13California constitutional rule: assessed value rises at most 2% a year and is reset to market value on a change in ownership.BOE Proposition 13 · Cal. Const. art. XIII A
Proposition 19California rule (in force since 2021): inherited property is reassessed at full market value unless the heir moves in and claims the parent-child exclusion within the deadlines; a limited allowance offsets part of the reassessment.BOE Proposition 19 · R&TC 63.2
Proposition 19 allowanceAn amount (started at $1,044,586, adjusted every second February by California house-price change) that offsets the reassessment when an inherited home is not the heir's principal residence.BOE LTA 2025/009 · BOE LTA 2026/026
Homeowners' exemption$7,000 of taxable value knocked off the assessment of a principal residence. Only one home can have it.BOE Homeowners' Exemption
Change in ownershipThe date ownership transfers (for a revocable trust, generally the date of death). It triggers reassessment and must be reported to the Assessor within 150 days.BOE Rule 462.260 · R&TC 480
Supplemental tax billA bill issued after the Assessor reassesses, covering the period from the change in ownership.county practice; no single official page confirmed flag U06 Santa Clara tax rate book
Federal estate taxTax on the gross estate above the exclusion. The 2026 exclusion is $15,000,000; the top rate is 40%.Rev. Proc. 2026-35
California estate / inheritance taxCalifornia does not impose an estate tax or an inheritance tax.CA State Controller
Net investment income tax (NIIT)3.8% federal tax on certain investment income for higher earners.IRS Topic 559
Depreciation recapturePart of a rental building's cost is written off each year; on sale, up to 25% federal tax can apply to that write-off.IRS Publication 527 · IRS Topic 409
SALT capLimit on deducting state and local taxes on a federal return ($10,000 under current law; temporarily higher through 2029 under P.L. 119-21).IRS Schedule A instructions
Reinvestment riskThe risk that money taken out of an investment (here, sale proceeds parked in Treasuries) must be reinvested later at a lower return, or that staying out of stocks means missing gains while waiting.site's own usage - see Decision page and Black swan section Treasury yields
Dollar-cost averaging (DCA)Investing a fixed amount at regular intervals instead of all at once. Used in the phased-investing path on the Black swan page.site's own usage - no official definition cited Treasury yields
Treasury bill / noteShort- to medium-term U.S. government debt. Bills mature in a year or less; notes in 2 to 10 years. Yields are published daily by the Treasury.U.S. Treasury yields
Inflation (CPI-U)The Bureau of Labor Statistics' Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers - the measure used for Proposition 13's annual inflation factor (capped at 2%).BLS CPI-U (FRED)
FHFA house-price indexThe Federal Housing Finance Agency's index of home prices from actual sales, used here for San Jose, San Francisco and California home-price history.FHFA HPI - San Jose (FRED) · FHFA HPI - San Francisco (FRED)
HUD Small Area Fair Market RentHUD's 40th-percentile rent standard for a ZIP code, used here as a rent benchmark for Property A - a benchmark, not a verified rent for this home.HUD SAFMR 95014 · flag A07
Property A / Property BPlaceholders: Property A = the Cupertino home to be inherited (ZIP 95014 withheld); Property B = the San Francisco three-unit home already owned (ZIP 94122 withheld).site's own placeholders - privacy control BOE Prop 19
the Transferor / the InheritorPlaceholders for the current owner of Property A and the person who already owns Property B and will inherit Property A.site's own placeholders - privacy control IRS estate tax
