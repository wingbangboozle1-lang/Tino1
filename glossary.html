Glossary

Plain-English meanings of the terms used on this site, each linked to its official page.

Terms marked model term are this site's own words for the analysis, not legal or tax terms. Read each definition as a pointer to the official page, not as a full statement of the law. The attorney and the tax adviser decide how a term applies to this trust.

Assessed value
The value on which California property tax is calculated. It is not the market value; it is the base year value, adjusted each year under Proposition 13. BOE Prop 19
Basic exclusion amount
The amount of a person's estate that is exempt from federal estate tax. It is $15,000,000 for deaths in 2026 under the federal statute as amended in 2025; it is indexed for inflation after 2026. IRS estate tax · IRS what's new
Basis (stepped-up)
The starting figure for measuring a capital gain. For inherited property the basis is generally its fair market value on the date of death, or on the alternate valuation date if one is elected. IRS inherited-property FAQ
Base year value
The value fixed when a property last changed ownership. Under Proposition 13 it is adjusted by an inflation factor each year, capped at 2% a year. R&TC 51
Break-even
A model term: the value of one input at which two paths give the same result, with every other input held at its baseline. model term - no official page
Black swan
A model term: a rare, extreme sequence of economic conditions outside the baseline. On this site it is shown through real historical replays and named stress paths, not as a forecast. model term - no official page
Change in ownership
A transfer that causes property to be reassessed to its current market value under California law, unless an exclusion applies. A transfer at death is a change in ownership. R&TC 480 · BOE Prop 19
CPI-U
The Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers, published by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. It is the common measure used to turn nominal dollars into real dollars. BLS CPI (FRED)
Depreciation (residential rental)
Yearly deduction for the building part of a rental property, spread over 27.5 years. It reduces taxable rent but is taxed back on a later sale. IRS Pub. 527
Earthquake insurance
Separate cover for earthquake damage. Standard homeowners policies generally do not include earthquake damage; the California Department of Insurance explains the options. CDI earthquake guide
Empty homes tax (San Francisco)
A San Francisco tax on certain vacant residential property. Whether it applies to Property B is an open question, not an assumption on this site. SF Empty Homes Tax
Estate tax
A federal tax on the transfer of a large estate at death. California has no estate tax for deaths after 2004. CA Controller · IRS estate tax
Form 706
The federal estate tax return. It is due nine months after death; a six-month extension is available on Form 4768. Form 706 instructions · IRS estate FAQ
Form 1041
The income tax return for an estate or trust. It is required when the estate or trust has gross income of $600 or more in a year. IRS estate income tax
Gross estate
The property counted for federal estate tax at death. Whether Property A is in the Transferor's gross estate depends on the trust type and is an open question (Q01). Rev. Rul. 2023-2
Grantor trust (irrevocable)
A trust whose assets may fall outside the estate for estate tax. Under Revenue Ruling 2023-2, a basis step-up generally does not apply to such assets. Rev. Rul. 2023-2
Homeowners' exemption
A $7,000 reduction in assessed value for an owner-occupied principal residence, claimed on form BOE-266. BOE homeowners' exemption
Homeowners insurance
Cover for the building and contents of an owner-occupied home. Premiums are set by each insurer; the California Department of Insurance publishes guides and a premium survey. CDI residential guide · CDI premium survey
Net-metering (NEM)
California billing for rooftop solar that credits exported power against the bill. The rules of the tariff set how long a credit is carried. CPUC net metering
Net investment income tax (NIIT)
A 3.8% federal tax on certain investment income above income thresholds. It can apply to a gain on a sale. IRS Topic 559
Parent-child exclusion (Proposition 19)
The rule that lets a child keep a parent's assessed value on a family home, up to the excluded amount, if the child makes it a principal residence within a year and claims the exclusion within three years. BOE Prop 19 · Santa Clara Assessor
Phased investing
A model term: moving cash into stocks in equal tranches over several years rather than all at once. Tested on the phased-investing page. model term - no official page
Portability
Transfer of a deceased spouse's unused federal exclusion to the surviving spouse. It requires a timely Form 706 return. IRS estate FAQ
Probate notice (trust)
The duty of a successor trustee to notify beneficiaries and heirs after a trust becomes irrevocable at death. The statute sets a 60-day period. Prob. Code 16061.7
Proposition 13
The 1978 California constitutional amendment. It caps the property tax rate at 1% of assessed value plus voter-approved debt, and limits assessed-value growth to 2% a year. R&TC 51
Real vs nominal
Nominal means the dollars of that year. Real means the same amount expressed in dollars of the start year, using CPI. The site shows both. BLS CPI (FRED)
Reinvestment risk
A model term: the risk that proceeds, or cash waiting to be invested, earn less than the planned return. The what-if page shows it next to the headline. model term - no official page
Revocable trust
A trust the creator can change or revoke while alive. Its assets are generally counted in the creator's estate; the trust terms and the attorney decide the specifics. Rev. Rul. 2023-2
Rent control (San Francisco)
Limits on yearly rent increases for covered units, set by the San Francisco Rent Board. The limit is tied to inflation. SF rent increases
Safmr (HUD small-area fair market rent)
A federal benchmark rent by ZIP code, published by HUD. It is a benchmark only; it is not a quote for any property. HUD SAFMR [ZIP A] · HUD SAFMR [ZIP B]
Secured property tax
The annual property tax on real estate, secured by the land. In San Francisco the rate for the year is published by the Treasurer and Tax Collector. SF Treasurer
Sequence risk
A model term: the risk that the order of good and bad years, not just the average, decides the outcome. It is why the replays use real sequences. model term - no official page
Section 121 exclusion
Exclusion of part of a gain on a principal residence: $250,000 for a single owner, $500,000 for a married couple filing jointly, if the owner-use test is met. IRS Topic 701
Step-up in basis
See Basis (stepped-up). IRS inherited-property FAQ
Supplemental assessment
An extra property tax bill for the period after a change in ownership, when the new value is higher than the old one. SF Treasurer
Tax rate book
The county document that sets the property tax rates for each area for the year. Santa Clara tax rate book
Treasury bill, note, bond
U.S. government debt. Bills mature within a year; notes run two to ten years; bonds longer. Yields change daily; the Treasury publishes them. Treasury yields
Unrecaptured section 1250 gain
The part of a gain on depreciated real estate that is taxed at up to 25% federally. IRS Topic 409
