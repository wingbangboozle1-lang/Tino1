Glossary

Plain-language meanings of the legal, tax, property and money terms used on this site, each with its official page.

Terms in the glossary appear in the notes across the site. Use the links to read the governing page before relying on a term. "Black swan" is not listed: no official definition was located, so the term is explained on the Black swan page as this site uses it (flag A19).

Base-year value (assessed value)The taxable value of a property for California property tax. Starts at the purchase or transfer price and rises at most 2% a year.Property A and Property B assessed values on the projections.R&TC 51

Fixed monthly chargeA flat monthly charge on an electricity bill, not tied to use. Relevant to a solar home that is a net credit.Electricity line for Property A (solar).CPUC fixed charge

Gross estateEverything the decedent owned or controlled at death, counted before debts and expenses. Determines the estate tax.Whether Property A is in the estate decides the basis reset.IRS estate tax
Homeowners' exemptionA California reduction in assessed value for an owner-occupied home, currently $7,000 of assessed value.Property A if the Inheritor moves in.BOE homeowners' exemption
Net investment income taxA federal 3.8% tax on investment income above certain thresholds.Included in the investment and sale tax rates.IRS Topic 559
Parent-child exclusion (Proposition 19)California rule that lets a child keep the parent's assessed value only if the child moves in as the principal residence and claims it in time.The central property-tax choice for Property A.BOE Prop 19
PortabilityCarrying a deceased spouse's unused federal exclusion to the survivor. Requires a timely Form 706.Estate room if a spouse died earlier.IRS estate FAQ
Principal residence and the two-year testA home lived in as the main home for at least two of the five years before a sale may exclude up to a set amount of gain.Live-then-sell mixed paths (flag A16).IRS Topic 701
Proposition 13California's property-tax limit: assessed value resets to market value only on a change in ownership, and rises at most 2% a year.Why Property B's assessed value stays far below its market value.R&TC 51
Reinvestment riskThe chance that money must be put back to work at lower returns than planned, or in a bad market.The worst-of-50 column and the phased paths.SEC diversification guide
Rent-controlled unit (San Francisco)A unit covered by the San Francisco Rent Ordinance, with annual increases capped by the Rent Board.Rent growth on Property B's let units.SF rent increases
