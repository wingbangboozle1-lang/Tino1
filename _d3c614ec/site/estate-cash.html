ESTATE & CASH HEADROOM · SECTION 07

Cash Inheritance & Estate Tax Thresholds

Federal, California, and local estate/inheritance tax exemptions, exact cash headroom alongside Property A through 2050, portability doubling, and legislative-cut stress tests.

Key Threshold Facts Verified on 2026-11-11 • Federal Estate & Gift Basic Exclusion: $15,000,000 per decedent in 2026 (P.L. 119-21 § 70106) and $15,480,000 in 2027 (IRS Rev. Proc. 2026-35 § 3.44); amounts above the exclusion are taxed at 40% (IRC § 2001(c)). Note that the exclusion applies to the Transferor's total gross estate + lifetime taxable gifts, not per beneficiary.
• California & Local Death Taxes: $0 California estate or inheritance tax for deaths on/after Jan 1, 2005 (CA State Controller), and $0 county/city documentary transfer tax on inheritances (CA R&TC § 11930).
• Cash Account Character Warning (F-07): Outright cash and stepped-up taxable brokerage accounts carry $0 income tax at receipt, whereas pre-tax traditional IRAs/401(k)s are subject to ordinary federal + CA income tax as distributed under the 10-year rule.
