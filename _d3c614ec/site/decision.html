DECISION ENGINE · SECTION 03

Decision Analysis: Live vs. Rent vs. Sell & Reinvest

Like-for-like after-tax comparison across equities, Treasury bills/savings, renting, and owner move-in, including exact Prop 19 reassessment math and the IRS Rev. Rul. 2023-2 basis check.

Gate #1 Before Executing Any Sale: Verify Trust Inclusion Under IRS Revenue Ruling 2023-2 (F-04) If Property A sits inside a standard revocable living trust (included in the Transferor's gross estate under IRC §§ 2036/2038), IRC § 1014 resets income-tax basis to $6,531,230 at a 2045 death, so a prompt sale owes $0 capital-gains tax. If instead it sits in an irrevocable grantor trust excluded from the gross estate, Rev. Rul. 2023-2 denies the step-up and triggers $1,947,760 in immediate federal + CA capital-gains taxes upon a 2045 sale.
