Why Separating Ad Valorem Tax from Fixed Parcel Charges Matters

Dividing Property B's $2,440 bill by its $114,250 assessed value yields an apparent 2.14% ratio only because voter-approved flat parcel taxes (~$1,110–$1,170/yr) sit on top of a small Prop 13 base. Separating the statutory ad valorem rate from fixed parcel charges prevents overstating post-reassessment tax bills.
