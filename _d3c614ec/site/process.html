Step-by-Step Trust Inheritance Roadmap

Clear statutory sequence from pre-death trust verification through 60-day trustee notices, 150-day Santa Clara County filings, 9-month IRS Form 706 deadlines, Prop 19 windows, and proceeds deployment.

Date of Change in Ownership for Trust Transfers Under BOE Property Tax Rule 462.260(d)(1), when a revocable living trust becomes irrevocable at the settlor's death, all statutory property-tax clocks (150-day R&TC § 480(b) notice, 1-year occupancy & BOE-266, and 3-year BOE-19-P) run from the date of death — not the later date a deed is recorded.
