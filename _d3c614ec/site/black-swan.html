Why Variable Regimes Matter (Kept Separate from Baseline Projections) Real economies never grow at a smooth 4.0% or 7.0% every year. In downturns, California's Proposition 8 (CA R&TC § 51(a)(2)) temporarily lowers reassessed property taxes when market value drops below factored base year value, whereas high inflation (1977–1982) erodes real Prop 13 tax burdens while pushing up insurance and repair costs. All simulations below include automatic Prop 8 reductions.
