Chronological statutory roadmap from pre-inheritance trust verification through county recording, IRS basis documentation, Prop 19 deadlines, and reinvestment execution.

Two Non-Negotiable Deadlines Upon Inheritance: (1) 150 Days: County Assessor notification (Form BOE-502-D under CA RTC § 480(b)). (2) 365 Days (1 Year): Physical move-in + BOE-266 Homeowners' Exemption + BOE-19-P filing if claiming the Proposition 19 partial reassessment exclusion on [Property A — Cupertino 95014] (missing the 1-year deadline forfeits retroactive relief back to date of death!).
