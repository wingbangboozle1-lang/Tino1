Cash + Real Estate Inheritance Thresholds, Trust Step-Up & Legislative Outlook

Exact federal estate tax cash-headroom projections (2026–2050), California $0 death-tax statutes, Revocable vs. Irrevocable Trust basis mechanics, and policy change stress tests.

How Much Money + Real Estate Can Be Inherited Tax-Free? Under current law enacted in Public Law 119-21 § 70106 (OBBBA) and confirmed in IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32, the 2026 Federal Estate & Gift Tax Basic Exclusion is $15,000,000 per individual ($30,000,000 for a married couple filing Form 706 to elect Deceased Spousal Unused Exclusion [DSUE] portability), indexed for inflation starting in 2027. California State and local governments (Cupertino, Santa Clara County, San Francisco) impose $0 inheritance tax and $0 estate tax at any dollar threshold (CA RTC § 13301). Above the federal threshold, the estate pays a flat 40% marginal tax on the excess.

Legislative & Policy Proposals Monitored Through 2040–2050

Policy / Proposal Area Potential Change Discussed Impact on Decision Plan & Mitigation Official Reference
Federal Estate Exemption Rollback Future Congresses have proposed reducing the basic exclusion back to $3,500,000–$7,000,000 and raising top rates to 45%–55%. Under a $7,000,000 rollback (indexed to ~$10,680,000 in 2045), a single parent owning Cupertino ($6,774,050) would have only $3,900,000 of cash headroom before 40%+ estate tax. Mitigation: File Form 706 DSUE portability at first spouse's death and gift high-basis cash/treasuries first, never low-basis real estate. IRS Form 706 DSUE · OBBBA P.L. 119-21
Repeal or Cap on IRC § 1014 Step-Up at Death Periodic federal budget proposals have floated taxing unrealized gains at death above a $5,000,000 per-person exclusion (with primary-residence exemptions). If § 1014 were capped, holding vs. selling would face symmetrical realization rules, increasing the relative value of deferring sale via living in Cupertino (S2B). Monitor federal tax bills prior to 2040. IRS Topic 409 · IRS Rev. Rul. 2023-2
California Prop 19 Amendment / Repeal Initiatives Taxpayer groups have repeatedly sought ballot initiatives to restore pre-2021 Prop 58 parent-child exclusions (while other proposals target Prop 13 commercial rolls). If Prop 58 were ever restored by CA voters before 2040–2050, Cupertino's parent-child primary residence tax would stay near ~$10,630/yr (and up to $1,000,000 of assessed rental value could be excluded), dramatically improving S2 and S3. Under current law, plan strictly for Prop 19. CA BOE Pub 801 · CA BOE LTA 2025/015
California Wealth / Estate Tax Proposals Periodic CA legislative bills (e.g., proposed wealth taxes or estate taxes above $15,000,000–$30,000,000) require statewide voter repeal of Prop 6 (RTC § 13301). Currently not enacted ($0 tax); any future ballot measure would appear on a statewide November election ballot with advance planning runway. CA RTC § 13301
