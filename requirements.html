Owner figures and assumptions, and why each is flagged

These are the only places where a number in this site does not come from an official page. Each is listed on the Flagged for Review page with what would replace it.

  • Repairs and maintenance: $6,000 a year for Property A and $12,000 for Property B - No official figure exists for a particular house. The lower figure for Property A is the owner's statement that it needs less work than Property B. flag A08
  • Home prices rise 4.0% a year and prices in both areas move together - The FHFA index gives history, not a forecast. The long-run California rate is shown next to the assumption on the sources page. flag A01
  • Inflation 2.5% a year; rents, insurance, water and electricity each grow at a stated margin above it - The 1976-2025 record in Black swan shows how wrong a constant rate can be, which is why that page exists. flag A01
  • Stocks 7.0%, Treasury bonds 4.5%, savings or bills 3.5%, dividend yield 1.5% - Ten-year Treasury yields are official; the equity return and the spread over bonds are not. flag A02
  • Home insurance $1,500 and landlord insurance $1,500 a year - The owner's estimate. The official statewide figures are the only ZIP-free numbers that exist; a real quote is needed. flag U01
  • The fixed charges inside each tax bill ($930 for Property A, $1,090 for Property B) - Derived by subtracting the official rate times the assessed value from the owner's rounded bill. The exact tax rate area was deliberately not looked up. flag U03
  • The Proposition 19 allowance after 2027-02-16 - The Board of Equalization publishes the next figure in February 2027; the model assumes it grows with the FHFA California index. flag U04
  • Selling cost 5%, vacancy 5%, management 6%, building share 30%, rent growth and rent of the third unit at Property B - Market conventions, not official figures. Each is shown on the decision page so it can be changed. flag A06
  • The trust type, the amount of cash and other assets, and whether a deceased spouse's exclusion was ported - Owner answers needed. These three decide the basis reset and the estate-tax bill. flag Q01

Rules this site holds itself to, where there is no external fact to check

  • No personal information. No name, street address, parcel number, e-mail or phone number appears. The people are "the Transferor" and "the Inheritor". final/check.py scans every published page on every build for an address, e-mail, phone or parcel pattern, for unrounded owner figures, and for the one figure the owner asked to be hidden - the build fails if any appears.
  • Every figure to the nearest $10. Owner-supplied and modelled dollars are rounded; a short list of figures that an official page publishes unrounded (an index value, a statutory cap such as $1,044,586) is quoted exactly and listed on the sources page.
  • No manual input. Three scripts produce the whole site from two data files: final/model.py (the model), final/build.py (the pages) and final/check.py (the rules above). Nothing on any page is typed by hand.
  • Every table row links to an official page, and a value that could not be confirmed is left blank and listed on the Flagged for Review page rather than estimated.
  • A dashboard, not an essay. Summary first, then one page per question, then the year-by-year tables. Tables scroll sideways on a phone, the navigation stays at the top, and every page loads without any outside script or font.
  • One front door, with earlier work kept. Parallel work sessions produced several editions; the earlier ones are preserved under Earlier versions with a notice, and nothing was deleted.
  • Nothing published until it builds. The pull request, the checks and the Pages deployment are recorded in COORDINATION.md; the build is refused if a link breaks or a row loses its source.

One privacy item cannot be fixed from inside the site: this repository is public, and its Git history holds the first drafts, written before the figures were rounded. Rounding the pages does not rewrite that history. The owner has to make the repository private or have the history rewritten. flag P02

What this pass changed

This page is new. It adds no figures: every number on it is produced by final/model.py or quoted from an official page linked in the row. The rest of the site is unchanged, and the checks below were run on the whole tree after the page was added. If a later pass changes a figure, this page is regenerated with the rest of the site, so it cannot drift out of date.

  • Build: python3 final/model.py && python3 final/build.py - twelve substantive pages, plus the earlier-versions page and redirects at retired addresses.
  • Checks: python3 final/check.py . - every table row carries a link to an official page, every internal link and anchor resolves, every dollar figure is a multiple of $10 unless it is a listed official figure, and no personal information or unrounded owner figure appears.
  • Model self-tests: the Proposition 19 formula is checked against the county assessor's own worked example, and Property B is asserted never to be reassessed.
