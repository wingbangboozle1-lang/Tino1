What-if controls: change the assumptions and see the answer move

Pick a home-price path, a stock return, a rent and a parking yield. The best choice, the wealth figures and the reinvestment risk update together, with no value outside the model grid.

What this page does. The four controls pick one of 54 combinations that the model has already run in full. No value is calculated in the browser beyond a lookup, so every figure is traceable to the model run. The controls show the range the brief asked for: home prices, stock returns, Property A rent and the parking yield each take three or two values, and the baseline is the middle value (the parking yield baseline is the model's 3.5% assumption). These are constructions for comparison, not forecasts. Flags flag H01 and flag A01 apply.

Best under these controls (baseline path)

Sell and reinvest, all at once gives the most wealth under these controls: $9,695,400 in 2026 dollars.

Reinvestment risk, beside the headline

All at once loses $2,612,220 (-27%) if a 2008-type bust begins in the inheritance year, and $4,938,650 (-51%) in a 1929-type Depression. Parking for 24 months changes those two results by $1,714,150 and $5,273,190.
