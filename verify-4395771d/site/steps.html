How the inheritance works, step by step

For a home held in a living trust that passes to a child at the parent's death. Each step links to the official page it was read on.

The one that costs the most if missed

To keep part of the low assessed value the heir must be living in the home within one year of the death and must have filed the Homeowners' Exemption claim by then. The Board of Equalization states there are no exceptions. On a 2045 inheritance the difference is about $17,600 of property tax in the first year, growing about 2% a year for as long as the heir lives there. BOE Letter to Assessors 2026/026, Q7
