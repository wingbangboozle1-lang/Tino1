Flagged for Review

Everything that could not be confirmed on an official page, every assumption the model makes, and the irregularities found while reviewing this repository. None of it blocks the site; all of it is for the owner to review.

Privacy

  • No name, street address, parcel number or trust identifier appears on this site, and none was looked up.
  • Owner-supplied dollar figures are shown rounded to the nearest $10, as instructed.
  • Rounding to $10 prevents an exact-number web search from matching. It does not hide the figures from someone who holds a full assessment roll. If stronger protection is wanted, the year-by-year history can be removed without changing any result.
  • The site asks search engines not to index it and loads nothing from other servers.
