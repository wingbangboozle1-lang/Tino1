How to read these tables

  • Assessed value rises by at most 2% a year while the same owner holds the property. BOE Letter to Assessors 2026/002

  • Insurance, water and maintenance start from the owner's figures or stated assumptions and grow with inflation plus the margins listed under assumptions.
