The second model, its own source register and its pages are kept as an independent check. It is not a second front door; its figures are quoted from the coordination log of 2026-11-11 04:58 UTC.

Method

  • One model. final/model.py projects both homes year by year from 2026 to 2076 and compares three choices for Property A from the year of inheritance. It uses only the Python standard library.
  • Like-for-like comparison. Each choice has an investment account that receives every dollar the choice produces or costs, invested the same way. At each year-end the model works out what would be left if everything were sold and all taxes paid.
  • Property B is never inherited in the model. Its assessed value, basis and rent continue unchanged. It enters the comparison only through the option of letting the third unit when the Inheritor moves to Property A: the baseline counts no rent for the third unit (nothing is inferred), and the letting upside appears only as one labelled scenario in the sensitivity table.
  • Property tax. Assessed values rise with inflation up to 2% a year and never exceed market value. At inheritance Property A is set to market value, or to the Proposition 19 partial value if lived in.
  • Income tax. Yearly income at 24% federal, 9.3% California and the 3.8% investment-income tax. One-off sales at 20% + 3.8% + 13.3%. Depreciation taken on a rental is taxed back at 25% federal on sale.
  • Non-constant paths. Real recorded years 1976-2025 are replayed in order from each possible start; named paths insert real crisis years into the baseline.
  • Rounding and privacy. Owner figures are stored only rounded to $10. No names, addresses or parcel numbers are used. Search engines are asked not to index the pages.
  • Checks. final/check.py fails the build if a table row has no official link, if an internal link is broken, if a dollar figure is not a multiple of $10 (official published figures excepted), or if a street address, e-mail address, parcel-number or telephone pattern appears. No fingerprint of the unrounded owner figures is stored in the repository; that extra scan runs only when the owner supplies the figures in a private local file.

Reproduce

python3 final/model.py && python3 final/build.py && python3 final/check.py
