Attorney &amp; CPA Briefing Sheet

A single printable page for the estate attorney and the tax adviser: the question, the figures, the questions to answer, the deadlines and what would change the answer.

Print at letter size; the page is set to fit on one sheet.

Question. After the Transferor's death, should the Inheritor live in Property A, rent it out, or sell it and reinvest, and should the proceeds be invested at once or phased in? Property B is already owned and is not part of the inheritance.

Answer on the numbers, if the basis resets to the date-of-death value. Selling beats renting in 43 of 50 real starting years after 10 years, and in 50 of 50 by 2076 (inheritance in 2045, baseline). Phasing the sale over 24 months beats selling at once in only 9 of 50 starting years after 10 years, but it softens the 2008-type and 1929-type entries (see the Mixed and phased paths page). Both points are conditional on the basis reset and the flags below.

Questions for the estate attorney

  1. Is Property A held by a trust that is included in the Transferor's gross estate, so the date-of-death value becomes the basis? (Q01)
  2. What is the total gross estate, and how much federal exclusion is left after cash and other assets? (Q02)
  3. Is a Form 706 filed for a predeceased spouse, and is portability available? (Q02)
  4. How should the trust distribute Property A, and is any sale or transfer to a third party planned?
  5. Does Property B need any trust or deed change so that it is untouched by the plan?

Questions for the CPA / tax adviser

  1. What is the Inheritor's filing status and bracket, and is the sale-year tax rate as modelled? (Q05)
  2. Is a two-year residence test met before any live-then-sell path, and what is the §121 exclusion? (flag A16)
  3. How is depreciation recapture computed if Property A is rented first? (A16)
  4. How would interest on Treasuries parked after a sale be reported, and is a 24- or 36-month schedule workable? (A14, A15)
  5. What is the Schedule A and Form 8971 treatment, and what basis is reported? (Process step 13)

What would change the answer. (1) The trust is outside the gross estate (no basis reset) (Q01). (2) The Inheritor moves in and claims the parent-child relief in time, so the property tax stays low (BOE-P19). (3) A rate, exclusion or property-tax rule changes before the inheritance (flag A13). (4) Stock returns well below the 7% baseline for the first decade (see What-if controls).
