# Executive summary — privacy-first inheritance decision guide

The public guide compares a potential trust transfer with a separate property the inheritor already owns. A transfer of the first property does not, by itself, change the ownership or tax basis of the second. The public site deliberately excludes personal financial inputs, property-specific projections and derived results: rounding alone cannot guarantee anonymity. Its [decision framework](privacy-site/public/decision.html), [year-by-year outlook](privacy-site/public/projection.html), [black-swan section](privacy-site/public/stress.html), [inheritance roadmap](privacy-site/public/process.html), [law](privacy-site/public/law.html), [costs](privacy-site/public/costs.html), and [flags](privacy-site/public/flagged.html) explain what is known and unknown.

## Decision and tax considerations

| Topic | Current position | Official context |
|---|---|---|
| Live, rent, or sell | No financial winner is established from verified public evidence. Compare incremental, after-tax cash flows and liquidity under comparable horizons. | [IRS rental expenses](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc414); [IRS inherited property](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/frequently-asked-questions-on-estate-taxes) |
| Family-home exclusion | Eligibility depends on the relationship, residence, claim timing, value limits and law in force at transfer; it is not automatic. | [California BOE](https://www.boe.ca.gov/pdf/pub800-1.pdf) |
| Income-tax basis | Some inherited property receives a date-of-death basis adjustment; an irrevocable grantor trust excluded from the estate can have a different result. | [IRS Revenue Ruling 2023-2](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2023-16_IRB#REV-RUL-2023-2) |
| Federal basic estate exclusion | $15,000,000 for 2026 deaths and $15,480,000 for 2027 deaths. These are estate-level basic exclusions, not per-beneficiary allowances. | [IRS 2027 adjustment](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-releases-tax-inflation-adjustments-for-tax-year-2027-taxpayers-continue-to-benefit-from-the-working-families-tax-cuts) |
| California estate tax | No California estate tax return is required for deaths on or after January 1, 2005, under current rules. | [California Controller](https://www.sco.ca.gov/ardtax_estate_tax.html) |
| Insurance | Premiums and coverage depend on property, occupancy and other factors; state comparison surveys are not quotes. | [California Department of Insurance](https://www.insurance.ca.gov/01-consumers/105-type/9-compare-prem/) |
| Solar | A bill credit does not necessarily equal a cash payout. Verify the applicable tariff and settlement. | [California PUC](https://www.cpuc.ca.gov/industries-and-topics/electrical-energy/demand-side-management/net-energy-metering) |
| Future outcomes | Home values, law, estate contents, expenses, yields and tax liabilities are unknown. Historical shocks are illustrations, not forecasts. | [Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis: house-price index](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/ATNHPIUS41940Q) |
