Mixed and phased paths

Mixed paths (live or rent for some years, then sell) and phased reinvestment (park the proceeds in Treasuries, then move into stocks over 24 or 36 months), tested against the 1929-type and 2008-type crashes.

How to read this page. Each row is a complete path for Property A, run through the same model and the same tax rules as the rest of the site. The baseline inherits in 2045. Phasing is tested on the 2008-type and 1929-type crash sequences and on all 50 real starting years. Figures are after tax, in 2026 dollars, and rounded to $10. Property B is the same in every row and is left out.

Caution on comparing tables: the replay rows carry the real return of each year, and the 1976-2025 record includes stock returns above the 7%% baseline in many years. Compare plans within one table; do not compare a replay median with a baseline figure.
