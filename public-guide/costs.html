Insurance: what a policy actually covers

Insurance is the largest unverified number in the whole analysis, and the one most likely to be wrong. The California Department of Insurance publishes a residential insurance guide that states what a standard policy covers and, more importantly, what it excludes. The questions below are the ones a quote must answer.

Six insurance questions, and the official answer to each
Insurance questionAnswerWhy it mattersOfficial source
Does a homeowners policy cover earthquake?No, not unless the policy or a separate policy includes itThe state insurance department publishes a separate earthquake guide; a standard policy excludes the perilCDI-RES
Does it cover flood?NoFlood needs a separate policy, typically through the federal programmeCDI-RES
What happens if the house is vacant?Cover can be excluded or restricted after a stated vacancy periodA long vacancy between inheriting and selling is a real gap in coverCDI-RES
Is a landlord policy the same product?NoLoss of rent, tenant liability and the actual occupancy must be disclosed. Using a homeowners policy for a let building can void a claimCDI-RES
How should a premium be obtained?A written quote for the actual occupancy and buildingZIP-code averages are marketing figures. The state publishes a premium comparison tool, and its own guide says to compare coverage, not only priceCDI-RES
