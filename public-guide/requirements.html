The four lines that were not answered the way they were asked

1. The bill history is not published

The brief asked for property tax and assessed values by year. That series is a fingerprint: a unique starting value with a 2% growth pattern identifies a parcel to anyone who has the county roll. The projection uses the current value only, and the history is recorded as a closed privacy flag (P03).

2. The exact assessed values are not published

The brief asked for the assessed value and the tax. This edition publishes the tax and the change in the assessment around the transfer, and withholds the standing assessed values, because they are the most searchable number in the whole file.

3. No insurance quote could be obtained

The brief asked for research into insurance costs. The research was done, and its conclusion is that no official source publishes a property-specific premium. Rather than substitute a ZIP-code average and call it a quote, the site keeps the figure labelled and flags it (U09).

4. The repository's history is not repaired

The brief asked for privacy to be the top priority. Every page is de-identified and the exact figures are out of the current files. The history, forks and caches are outside what a commit can fix, and making the repository private or rewriting history are owner decisions with irreversible effects (P02).

Nothing in this register is a self-assessment of quality. It records coverage: which line of the brief is answered, where, and how. The verification of each official page is on the sources page, and the things that could not be verified are on the Flagged page.

De-identification method. People, the trust, the parcels and the addresses appear only as placeholders and are never published. Household amounts are household or model outputs rounded to the nearest $10 and are labelled as such; statutory amounts are quoted exactly as the official page publishes them and are labelled official. No year-by-year bill history is published. Nothing on this site is legal, tax, investment or insurance advice.
