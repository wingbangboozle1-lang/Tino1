The inheritance-tax question, answered directly

Is there an inheritance tax?

No. California has no inheritance tax and no estate tax for deaths on or after 1 January 2005. The federal government taxes the estate, not the heir: the executor files, and the tax is paid before distribution. An heir who receives a distribution does not pay federal estate tax on it.

How much can be inherited before tax?

For a death in 2026 the federal exclusion is $15,000,000 of gross estate; for 2027 it is $15,480,000. Both are official amounts. Above that, the rate is 40% on the excess. The threshold applies to the whole estate, not to each beneficiary, and it applies after deductions for debts, expenses, charity and the marital deduction.
