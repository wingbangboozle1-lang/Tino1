Why these ranges

SettingRange offeredWhy this range, and how it is sourcedOfficial basis
Year of inheritance2040, 2045, 2050The trust is expected around 2040 to 2050 (the brief). The middle value is the baseline.BOE Prop 19
Stock return a year5%, 7%, 10%The baseline is 7%. Ten percent is the long-run S&P 500 total return the replay data uses; 5% is a low case. Not a forecast.NYU Stern returns table · SEC diversification guide
Inflation a year2.0%, 2.5%, 3.5%Official CPI history shows decades where inflation ran above and below 2.5% (see Black swan). Each setting also moves home growth and bill and bond rates by the same difference.BLS CPI (FRED) · BLS CPI release
Selling costs5%, 7%The baseline is 5% of the sale price. Seven percent tests a more expensive sale. No official figure exists for this range.Santa Clara Clerk-Recorder

What would change the answer

  • A different stock return moves the sell-and-invest result most: compare the 5% and 10% rows.
  • A higher inflation setting raises the home value and the property-tax base together, so it does not change the ranking by itself.
  • The estate and basis-reset facts (flags Q01 and Q02) decide whether the sell result is possible at all; no setting here changes that.
